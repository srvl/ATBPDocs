---
title: "Server splits (one plan, several servers)"
---

- A split divides your plan's RAM, CPU and disk into separate servers, each with its own console, files and SFTP. Typical use: a Velocity proxy, a lobby and one or more game servers.
- **32 GB and larger plans include free splits: 3 at 32 GB, 4 at 48 GB, 6 at 64 GB.** Each included split also adds ports and a database.
- Extra splits on any plan cost ₱20 per split per month, billed with your server (same 5/10/15% discounts on 3/6/12-month terms).
- Create and manage splits in the panel with the Server Splitter.
- Performance is the same as separate servers; the total cannot exceed your plan.
