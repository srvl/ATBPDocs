---
title: "Server splits (one plan, several servers)"
---

- A split divides your plan's RAM, CPU and disk into separate servers, each with its own console, files and SFTP. Typical use: a Velocity proxy, a lobby and one or more game servers.
- **32 GB and larger plans include free splits: 3 at 32 GB, 4 at 48 GB, 6 at 64 GB.** The plan's backup count already covers included splits, so they add no extra backups.
- Extra splits on any plan (games and bots) cost ₱20 per split per month, billed with your server (5% off on 6-month and 10% off on 12-month terms, like the plans). Each bought split adds 3 backup slots, and every split (free or bought) adds 3 ports and 1 database.
- Create and manage splits in the panel with the Server Splitter. A split can run your plan's game, a Discord bot or a database, or any other game your plan's size covers (the same rule as [switching games](/using_the_panel/basic-controls#switching-to-a-different-game)).
- Performance is the same as separate servers; the total cannot exceed your plan.
- **Dropping splits:** if you downgrade to fewer splits than you have split servers, the newest extra split servers are suspended. Delete one in the Server Splitter, or buy the split back, and open a ticket to have it started again.
