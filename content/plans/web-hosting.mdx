We host websites and databases on a server in Singapore, with the HestiaCP control panel. Your account backs up every night, with a copy kept off the server; the plan sets how many backups are kept. Order at https://billing.atbphosting.com/products/web-hosting.

| Plan | Storage | Websites | Databases | Cron jobs | Nightly backups kept | Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Web Starter | 5 GB | no cap | no cap | 5 | 1 | ₱49 |
| Web Plus | 20 GB | no cap | no cap | 20 | 3 | ₱129 |
| Web Pro | 50 GB | no cap | no cap | 50 | 7 | ₱249 |

- Paying for 3, 6 or 12 months at once saves 5%, 7.5% or 10%.
- Storage is the plan's limit. Websites and databases have no cap of their own, as long as they fit in your storage and follow the acceptable use policy.
- **Email is not included.** Our host blocks outgoing mail on port 25, so we do not run a mail server here. There are no mailboxes or webmail. To use email on your domain, use a mail provider and add its records at your DNS host.
- A domain is not included. Bring your own or register one with us.
- Plans are limited in number. If a plan shows out of stock, check back or ask in a ticket.

## Pointing your domain

At checkout you type the domain for your first website. We set up the website and request a free Let's Encrypt SSL certificate.

- **Domain registered with us:** we point it at your hosting for you (website and www records), so the site and SSL work without touching DNS. Records you already have for email elsewhere are kept.
- **Domain from another registrar:** add A records at your DNS host pointing to `213.35.96.233`, one for the domain and one for www. A subdomain such as `blog.example.com` needs only its own A record. The address is also on your service page.

## SSL

Until your domain points at the server, SSL shows as **Pending** on your service page. We retry every hour for two days, or press **Install SSL** once the domain points. A new certificate can take up to 2 minutes to appear on your site.

## Logging in

- Your service page in billing shows the control panel address, your panel user name, domain, plan and SSL status.
- Open the control panel with the **Open control panel** button. It signs you in once, without a password.
- To log in directly at https://web1.atbphosting.com:8087 or from an app, press **Generate new password**. The new password shows on your service page for 10 minutes, so save it then. We never email it.

## Running your site

- Sites run on nginx with PHP-FPM and MariaDB databases.
- `.htaccess` files do nothing here, because there is no Apache. For WordPress and other common apps, pick the matching web template for your domain in the panel so their links work.
- Change plan any time from your service page. Moving to a smaller plan never deletes anything; the panel only stops new items (files, cron jobs, backups) until you are under the new plan's limits.
- If a service is suspended for an unpaid invoice, your sites show a suspended page until it is paid. Nothing is deleted while suspended.
