We host websites, databases and email on our AMD EPYC 7B13 web server in Singapore, with the HestiaCP control panel. Order at https://billing.atbphosting.com under **Web Hosting**.

| Plan | Disk | Websites | Databases | Mailboxes | Cron jobs | Backups kept | Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Web Starter | 5 GB | no cap | no cap | no cap | 5 | 1 | ₱49 |
| Web Plus | 20 GB | no cap | no cap | no cap | 20 | 3 | ₱129 |
| Web Pro | 50 GB | no cap | no cap | no cap | 50 | 7 | ₱249 |

- Disk space is the plan's limit. Websites, databases and mailboxes have no cap of their own, as long as they fit in your disk space and follow the acceptable use policy.
- At checkout you type the domain for your first website. We set up the website and email for that domain and request a free Let's Encrypt SSL certificate.
- A domain is not included. Bring your own or register one with us.
- **Domain registered with us:** we point it at your hosting for you (website, www and email records), so the site and SSL work without touching DNS. Records you already have for email elsewhere are kept.
- **Domain from another registrar:** point the domain and www to the web server (A records; the address is on your service page). Until it points, SSL shows as Pending on your service page; we retry every hour for two days, or press **Install SSL** once the domain points.
- Your service page in billing shows the control panel address, your panel user name, domain, plan and SSL status.
- Open the control panel with the **Open control panel** button. It signs you in once, without a password. To log in directly or from an app, press **Generate new password**: the new password shows on your service page for 10 minutes, so save it then. We never email it.
- Each site runs on nginx with Apache and PHP-FPM, with MariaDB databases, so .htaccess files and common apps like WordPress work.
- Email: create mailboxes for your domain in the panel and read mail in webmail or any mail app. For a domain from another registrar, the panel shows the DNS records (MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC) to add at your DNS host so your mail is delivered.
- Change plan any time from your service page. Moving to a smaller plan never deletes anything; the panel only stops new items (files, cron jobs, backups) until you are under the new plan's limits.
- If a service is suspended for an unpaid invoice, your sites show a suspended page until it is paid. Nothing is deleted while suspended.
