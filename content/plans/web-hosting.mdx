Web hosting is **coming soon**. It is not on sale yet, and prices are announced when it opens. This page shows what the plans will include.

We host websites and databases on a server in Singapore, with the HestiaCP control panel. Your account backs up every night, with a copy kept off the server; the plan sets how many backups are kept.

| Plan | Storage | Websites | Databases | Cron jobs | Nightly backups kept |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Web Starter | 5 GB | no cap | no cap | 5 | 1 |
| Web Plus | 20 GB | no cap | no cap | 20 | 3 |
| Web Pro | 50 GB | no cap | no cap | 50 | 7 |

- Storage is the plan's limit. Websites and databases have no cap of their own, as long as they fit in your storage and follow the acceptable use policy.
- **Email is not included.** We do not run a mail server on this plan, and there are no mailboxes or webmail. To use email on your domain, use a mail provider and add its records at your DNS host.
- A domain is not included. Bring your own or register one with us.

## Pointing your domain

At checkout you type the domain for your first website. We set up the website and request a free Let's Encrypt SSL certificate.

- **Domain registered with us:** we point it at your hosting for you (website and www records), so the site and SSL work without touching DNS. Records you already have for email elsewhere are kept.
- **Domain from another registrar:** point the domain and www to the web server with A records. The address is on your service page.

## SSL

Until your domain points at the server, SSL shows as **Pending** on your service page. We retry every hour for two days, or press **Install SSL** once the domain points.

## Logging in

- Your service page in billing shows the control panel address, your panel user name, domain, plan and SSL status.
- Open the control panel with the **Open control panel** button. It signs you in once, without a password.
- To log in directly or from an app, press **Generate new password**. The new password shows on your service page for 10 minutes, so save it then. We never email it.

## Running your site

- Sites run on nginx with PHP-FPM and MariaDB databases.
- `.htaccess` files do nothing here, because there is no Apache. For WordPress and other common apps, pick the matching web template for your domain in the panel so their links work.
- Change plan any time from your service page. Moving to a smaller plan never deletes anything; the panel only stops new items (files, cron jobs, backups) until you are under the new plan's limits.
- If a service is suspended for an unpaid invoice, your sites show a suspended page until it is paid. Nothing is deleted while suspended.
