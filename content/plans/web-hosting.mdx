## Pointing your domain

At checkout you type the domain for your first website. We set up the website and request a free Let's Encrypt SSL certificate.

- **Domain registered with us:** we point it at your hosting for you (website and www records), so the site and SSL work without touching DNS. Records you already have for email elsewhere are kept.
- **Domain from another registrar:** add A records at your DNS host pointing to `213.35.96.233`, one for the domain and one for www. A subdomain such as `blog.example.com` needs only its own A record.
- Your service page shows **Domain pointing**: it checks your domain and tells you exactly which record is still missing.

## SSL

Until your domain points at the server, SSL shows as **Pending** on your service page. We retry every hour for two days, or press **Install SSL** once the domain points. A new certificate can take up to 2 minutes to appear on your site.

## Your service page does most of the work

Everything common is a button on your service page in billing, without learning the control panel:

- **Open file manager** signs you in and opens your files.
- **Install WordPress** installs it on your domain and shows the login once, for 10 minutes.
- **Host a Node.js app** runs a Node.js app on your domain.
- **Install SSL** requests your certificate again.
- **Open full control panel** signs you in to the rest of the tools: more websites, databases and cron jobs.
- **Generate new password** sets a new password, shown for 10 minutes. Use it to log in directly at https://web1.atbphosting.com:8087 or with an SFTP app. We never email it.

Uploading with an SFTP app: host `web1.atbphosting.com`, port `22`, your panel user name, and the password from **Generate new password**. Website files go in `web/your-domain/public_html`.

## Running your site

- Sites run on nginx with PHP and MariaDB databases, or as a Node.js app (Node.js 22).
- `.htaccess` files do nothing here, because there is no Apache. **Install WordPress** sets this up correctly by itself. For other apps, use **Quick install** in the panel or pick the matching web template for your domain.
- A Node.js app gets 512 MB on Web Starter, 1 GB on Web Plus and 2 GB on Web Pro. One Node.js app per account. Discord bots belong on [bot hosting](/plans/discord-bot-hosting): web plans are for websites.
- Change plan any time from your service page. Moving to a smaller plan never deletes anything; the panel only stops new items (files, cron jobs, backups) until you are under the new plan's limits.
- If a service is suspended for an unpaid invoice, your sites show a suspended page until it is paid. Nothing is deleted while suspended.
