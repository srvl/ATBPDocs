Budget+ is our low-cost line: ₱35 per GB of RAM at every size from 4 to 64 GB, on AMD EPYC 7B13 servers in Singapore, on sale since October 21, 2026. Every Budget+ plan is a fixed size: pick the RAM, and the vCPU, disk and backups come with it. The smallest Minecraft size is 4 GB (Terraria has its own 2 and 3 GB plans, and Valheim starts at 3 GB). Prices are in Philippine pesos; billing also shows a US dollar figure converted nightly.

Premium+ has faster cores, so it is the better pick for big modpacks, many plugins or 30+ players. Budget+ gives more RAM per peso for lighter servers. You can move from one to the other later from billing (see below). Premium+ sizes and prices are on [Premium+ plans and prices](/plans/premium-plus-plans-and-prices).

- Storage is 5 GB per 1 GB of RAM, with at least 50 GB on every plan. Every plan has at least 2 vCPU. Backups go up with size: 3 up to 6 GB, 4 for 8 to 12 GB, 5 for 16 to 24 GB, 6 from 32 GB.
- Each server starts with 1 database and 5 ports (its main port plus 4 extra). Need more? Ask in a Sales Inquiry ticket and we'll raise it. Every split, free or bought, adds 1 database and 3 ports. Free splits add no extra backups; the backups in the table already cover them.
- Longer terms are cheaper: 5% off for 6 months and 10% off for 12 months (3 months is the monthly price times three). There is no setup fee.
- 32 GB and larger plans include free server splits (3, 4 or 6). Any plan can add more splits for ₱20 each per month.
- Promo codes that work right now are on [Current promotions](/billing/current-promotions).
- Upgrade or downgrade any time from billing (Services > your server > **Upgrade** or **Downgrade**), to another Budget+ size or to Premium+. Picking Premium+ moves your server to the Premium+ machines by itself; your files come with you and the address changes. A server with Splits stays on Budget+ for now. See [Upgrading or downgrading](/billing/upgrades-and-downgrades).
- No player or plugin caps on Budget+. Normal fair use under the Terms still applies.
- Stock for Hytale, Palworld, FiveM, Terraria, Valheim and Project Zomboid on Budget+ is on [Stock right now](/plans/stock).

## Old Budget Micro and Budget Nano servers

The old Budget Nano and Budget Micro plans (ARM) are retired (October 22, 2026). They take no new orders and **cannot be renewed**.

- A Budget Micro server keeps running until the end of the term you already paid for. After that it is suspended and deleted like any unpaid server (see [Renewals, suspension and deletion](/billing/renewals-suspension-and-deletion)), and you can still download its files.
- To keep the server, use **Upgrade** on its service page before the term ends and move it to Budget+ or Premium+ (6 GB and bigger). You pay the difference for the days left, your files move with it, and the new plan renews as normal. There is no free move.
- Until the term ends, Budget Micro keeps its own rules: 20 GB of storage and 1 to 10 players. We recommend up to 10 plugins, and 20 plugins is the soft limit. A Budget Micro server with more than 20 plugins that slows down other servers on its node can be suspended, and it will not start until it is back under the limit. To run more, upgrade it to Budget+, which has no player or plugin caps.
