title: "Budget+ plans and prices (AMD EPYC 7B13)"

Budget+ is the low-cost line, running on AMD EPYC 7B13 servers in Singapore. It suits small and medium servers that don't need the single-core speed of Premium+. Every Budget+ plan is a fixed size. Prices are in Philippine pesos; billing also shows a US dollar figure converted nightly.

- Longer terms are cheaper: 5% off for 3 months, 10% off for 6 months, 15% off for 12 months. There is no setup fee.
- 32 GB and larger plans include free server splits (3, 4 or 6).
- There are no coupons or promo codes right now.
- Upgrade or downgrade to another Budget+ size any time from billing (Services > your server > Upgrade). Moving to Premium+ needs a ticket because it is a different machine; your files move with you.
- No player or plugin caps: Budget+ replaces the old Budget plan and its 10-player / 20-plugin limits. Normal fair use under the Terms still applies.
- The old Budget Micro plan (ARM) is retired when Budget+ launches.

**Budget+ or Premium+?** Premium+ has faster cores, so it is the better pick for big modpacks, many plugins or 30+ players. Budget+ gives more RAM per peso for lighter servers.
