Budget+ is the low-cost line at one price, ₱35 per GB of RAM at every size from 4 to 64 GB, running on AMD EPYC 7B13 servers in Singapore. It suits small and medium servers that don't need the single-core speed of Premium+. Every Budget+ plan is a fixed size. Prices are in Philippine pesos; billing also shows a US dollar figure converted nightly.

- Storage is 5 GB per 1 GB of RAM, with at least 50 GB on every plan (Premium+ gives 8 GB per 1 GB). Every plan has at least 2 vCPU. Backups go up with size: 3 up to 6 GB, 4 for 8 to 12 GB, 5 for 16 to 24 GB, 6 from 32 GB.
- Each server starts with 1 database and 5 ports (its main port plus 4 extra). Need more? Ask in a Sales Inquiry ticket and we'll raise it. Every split, free or bought, adds 1 database and 3 ports. Free splits add no extra backups; the backups in the table already cover them.
- Longer terms are cheaper: 5% off for 6 months and 10% off for 12 months (3 months is the monthly price times three). There is no setup fee.
- 32 GB and larger plans include free server splits (3, 4 or 6).
- Promo codes that work right now are on [Current promotions](/billing/current-promotions).
- Upgrade or downgrade any time from billing (Services > your server > **Upgrade** or **Downgrade**), to another Budget+ size or to Premium+. Picking Premium+ moves your server to the Premium+ machines by itself; your files come with you and the address changes. See [Upgrading or downgrading](/billing/upgrades-and-downgrades).
- No player or plugin caps on Budget+. Normal fair use under the Terms still applies.
- The old Budget Micro plan (ARM) no longer takes new orders now that Budget+ is on sale. Budget Micro servers have 20 GB of storage. Those that still renew keep that plan's rules: a first-month setup fee (not refunded) and a soft limit of 1 to 10 players and 10 plugins. That limit is not enforced strictly, as long as the server does not slow down the node for other customers.

**Budget+ or Premium+?** Premium+ has faster cores, so it is the better pick for big modpacks, many plugins or 30+ players. Budget+ gives more RAM per peso for lighter servers.

## Sizes for each game

Every game uses this same table and price, and every game below can be ordered on Budget+.

| Game | Budget+ sizes |
|---|---|
| Minecraft | 4 to 64 GB |
| Hytale | 6 to 32 GB |
| Palworld | 8 to 32 GB |
| FiveM | 4 to 16 GB |
| Terraria | 2 to 4 GB, see [Terraria plans](/plans/terraria-plans-and-prices) |

**Switching games:** a server can switch to any game whose smallest Budget+ size is at or below its plan, from **Settings > Change Game** on the panel. A smaller plan upgrades first and pays only the difference. See [Switching to a different game](/using_the_panel/basic-controls#switching-to-a-different-game).
