- There is no setup fee. Longer terms are cheaper: 5% off for 6 months and 10% off for 12 months, the same as game servers (3 months is the monthly price times three).
- At checkout pick the bot type: **Python Bot**, **NodeJS Bot**, **Red Bot**, **Drako Bot** (needs your own licence) or **Lavalink**.
- Your bot runs in the game panel (panel.atbphosting.com) with its own console, files and SFTP, like a game server. Upload your files, set the start file on the Startup tab, put your bot token in your own config, and start the server.
- Node.js bots run on **Nodejs 24** (the default) or **Nodejs 22**. Pick one as the Docker image on the Startup tab. Both get the current Node.js security updates. Older and end-of-life versions are no longer offered: bots that were on Node.js 20 moved to 22, and bots on 25 moved to 24, from their next restart. If your bot stops starting after that, update its packages (`npm update`), or open a ticket.
- If your bot or web app listens on a port (a dashboard, a webhook, Lavalink), use the port shown on the **Network** tab. Other ports are not reachable.
- Websites: there is no separate web hosting plan. Pick the **NodeJS Bot** type and serve your site with Node.js (for example Express serving your HTML files) on the port from the **Network** tab.
- Domains: a bot itself needs no domain. For a dashboard or web page your bot serves, create a free **Web App** subdomain in the panel (More > Subdomain) on that port, or use your own domain by pointing it at your server's IP in your domain's DNS. See [Custom domains and free subdomains](/general/custom-domains-and-subdomains).
- Python bots that read a `.env` file need `python-dotenv` in `requirements.txt`, and the `.env` file uploaded next to your start file.
- To run a bot and a Lavalink node, or several bots, on one plan, add server splits at checkout or later (₱20 per split per month). More backup slots are available as **Extra Backups**.
- MongoDB Atlas `mongodb+srv://` addresses work on every node. If your bot still fails with `queryTxt ESERVFAIL` or another DNS error, restart the server once, check that your Atlas network access list allows the connection, and open a ticket if it continues. The standard `mongodb://` connection string from Atlas (the one that lists every shard host and port) also works. You can also use the free MySQL database from the **Databases** tab if your bot supports it, see [MySQL Databases](/using_the_panel/databases).
- Never paste your bot token in a public channel or ticket. If it leaks, reset it in the Discord Developer Portal.

## Older bot plans

Basic Bot, Starter Bot, Intermediate Bot and Advanced Bot are no longer sold. A bot on one of them keeps its plan and its price and renews as before; nothing changes unless you change plan yourself.

To move to a new size, open **Services** > your bot > **Upgrade** or **Downgrade** in billing (see [Upgrades and downgrades](/billing/upgrades-and-downgrades)):

| Your plan today | Same RAM on the new plans | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Basic Bot (512 MB, ₱50) | Bot 512 MB, ₱50 | Same plan and price, nothing to change |
| Starter Bot (1 GB, ₱100) | Bot 1 GB, ₱90 | Downgrade (same size, cheaper) |
| Intermediate Bot (2 GB, 2 vCPU, ₱200) | Bot 2 GB, 1.5 vCPU, ₱170 | Downgrade |
| Advanced Bot (4 GB, 4 vCPU, ₱400) | Bot 4 GB, 2 vCPU, ₱300 | Downgrade |

Bigger sizes, up to Bot 8 GB, are under Upgrade. A downgrade applies straight away and the unused difference goes to your account credit. A bot still on the older Ryzen 9 9950X machines moves to Budget+ with its files when it changes plan; it gets a new address and port, which show in the panel.
