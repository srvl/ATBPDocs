We host Discord bots on AMD Ryzen 9 9950X machines in Singapore, running 24/7. Order one at https://billing.atbphosting.com under **Bots**.

| Plan | CPU | RAM | NVMe disk | Server splits | Extra ports | Backups | Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic Bot | 0.5 vCPU | 512 MB | 5 GB | 0 | 0 | 0 | ₱50 |
| Starter Bot | 1 vCPU | 1 GB | 10 GB | 1 | 1 | 0 | ₱100 |
| Intermediate Bot | 2 vCPU | 2 GB | 20 GB | 3 | 3 | 4 | ₱200 |
| Advanced Bot | 4 vCPU | 4 GB | 40 GB | 7 | 7 | 8 | ₱400 |

- Monthly billing has a one-time ₱50 setup fee on the first invoice. Longer terms skip it and cost less per month: 3 months at the monthly price, 6 months 5% off, 12 months 10% off (Basic Bot: ₱150, ₱285 or ₱540).
- At checkout pick the bot type: **Python Bot**, **NodeJS Bot**, **Red Bot**, **Drako Bot** (needs your own licence) or **Lavalink**.
- Your bot runs in the game panel (panel.atbphosting.com) with its own console, files and SFTP, like a game server. Upload your files, set the start file on the Startup tab, put your bot token in your own config, and start the server.
- Starter and larger plans include server splits, so one plan can run a bot and a Lavalink node, or several bots.
- Never paste your bot token in a public channel or ticket. If it leaks, reset it in the Discord Developer Portal.
