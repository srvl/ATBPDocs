---
title: "Backups"
description: "How backups work on ATBP game servers: how many you get with each plan, how to make and restore one, and what is kept."
---

- Every plan includes a set number of backup slots (see the plan tables: Budget+ and Premium+ 3 up to 6 GB, 4 for 8 to 12 GB, 5 for 16 to 24 GB, 6 from 32 GB; Terraria 3; bots 1 or 2). The free splits on 32 GB and larger plans do not add backups; each split you buy adds 3.
- Manage backups in the panel under **Backups**: create, download, restore or delete.
- We also take an automatic backup of every server each night, shown in **Backups** as "Auto Backup" with its date. A server that is stopped and whose files have not changed since its last backup is skipped that night, because that backup already has everything; it still gets a fresh one at least once a week. To get your own schedule, see [automatic backup creation](/using_the_panel/backups/guide#automatic-backup-creation).
- Need more slots? Add **Extra Backups** (+2, +5 or +10) from billing > Services > your server > **Upgrade**. To remove them, use **Downgrade**.
- On Budget+ servers and the old Budget Nano and Budget Micro servers, backups are kept only on our off-site backup storage, not on the game server machine. Restoring or downloading a big backup therefore can take a few minutes instead of seconds; leave the restore running until the panel says it is done.
- Backups are provided but not guaranteed. Keep your own copy of anything important by downloading a backup now and then (Terms section 6).
- When a server is deleted (for example 3 days after an unpaid due date), its panel backups are deleted too. One saved backup of its files, taken when it was suspended (or deleted), stays downloadable from billing > Services > the server for 30 days, and staff can restore the server from it within those 30 days if you open a ticket. See [suspensions and terminations](/billing/suspensions_and_terminations).
- Very frequent backup schedules (for example hourly on a big world) can cause lag. Daily is enough for most servers.
