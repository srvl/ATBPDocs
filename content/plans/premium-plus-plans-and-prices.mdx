- Disk is 10 GB of NVMe per 1 GB of RAM on every plan.
- Every plan includes 1 database and 5 extra ports. Each free split adds 3 more ports and 1 more database; the backups in the table already cover the free splits.
- Disk is not unlimited. If you need more than your plan gives, open a Sales Inquiry ticket.
- Longer terms are cheaper: 5% off for 3 months, 10% off for 6 months, 15% off for 12 months. There is no setup fee.
- 32 GB and larger plans include free server splits (3, 4 or 6) so you can run a proxy, lobby and game servers from one plan. Any plan can add more splits for ₱20 each per month.
- Dedicated-core plans are not offered right now.
- 48 and 64 GB plans are limited and often show out of stock. Open a Sales Inquiry ticket to be told when one opens.
- There are no coupons or promo codes right now. The term discounts above are the only discounts.
- You can upgrade or downgrade to another Premium+ size at any time from billing.atbphosting.com (Services > your server > Upgrade). Upgrades are charged pro rata for the rest of the cycle; downgrades are returned as account credit. You cannot switch between Premium+ and Budget+ from the billing page because they run on different machines; open a ticket for that.
