Premium+ runs on AMD Ryzen 9 9950X machines (up to 5.7 GHz, DDR5, storage in RAID 1) in Singapore. Every Premium+ plan is a fixed size from 6 GB (2 vCPU) up to 64 GB, at ₱75 per GB of RAM at every size: pick the RAM and the vCPU, disk and backups come with it. Prices are in Philippine pesos; billing also shows a US dollar figure converted nightly.

**Budget+ or Premium+?** Premium+ has faster cores, so it is the better pick for big modpacks, many plugins or 30+ players. [Budget+](/plans/budget-plus-plans-and-prices) (AMD EPYC 7B13, ₱35 per GB, from 4 GB) gives more RAM per peso for lighter servers; for example 8 GB is ₱600 a month on Premium+ and ₱280 on Budget+. Both are in Singapore and protected by TCPShield. A side-by-side table is on [Budget+ or Premium+?](/plans/budget-plus-plans-and-prices#budget-or-premium).

- Disk is 8 GB of storage per 1 GB of RAM on every plan. Backups go up with size: 3 up to 6 GB, 4 for 8 to 12 GB, 5 for 16 to 24 GB, 6 from 32 GB.
- Each server starts with 1 database and 5 ports (its main port plus 4 extra). Need more? Ask in a Sales Inquiry ticket and we'll raise it. Every split, free or bought, adds 1 database and 3 ports. Free splits add no extra backups; the backups in the table already cover them.
- Disk is fixed per plan. Each plan comes with the disk shown; for more, upgrade to a bigger plan. Servers on older plans keep the storage they have and can ask for more in a Sales Inquiry ticket when they need it.
- Longer terms are cheaper: 5% off for 6 months and 10% off for 12 months (3 months is the monthly price times three). There is no setup fee.
- 32 GB and larger plans include free server splits (3, 4 or 6) so you can run a proxy, lobby and game servers from one plan. Any plan can add more splits for ₱20 each per month.
- Dedicated-core plans are not offered right now.
- Premium+ starts at 6 GB. Servers already on a 3 or 4 GB Premium+ plan keep it, at the same price, until they change plan; smaller sizes are on Budget+ (from 4 GB) and the [Terraria plans](/plans/terraria-plans-and-prices).
- Servers on the older Premium+ plans where you picked the RAM and CPU at checkout keep that RAM and CPU and can still change splits and server software. To change size, they move to one of the plans above from **Upgrade** or **Downgrade**; see [Older Premium+ plans](/billing/upgrades-and-downgrades#older-premium-plans-where-you-picked-the-ram-and-cpu).
- The Stock column above is read from billing; stock for Hytale, Palworld, FiveM and every other line is on [Stock right now](/plans/stock). 48 and 64 GB plans are limited and often show sold out. Take the **Stocks** role on Discord to be pinged when stock opens.
- vCPU, RAM and storage are not sold one by one. Each size comes with the vCPU in the table; for more vCPU, move to a bigger size.
- Promo codes that work right now are on [Current promotions](/billing/current-promotions). With none listed, the term discounts above are the only discounts.
- You can upgrade or downgrade to another Premium+ size at any time from billing.atbphosting.com (Services > your server > **Upgrade** or **Downgrade**). Upgrades are charged pro rata for the rest of the cycle; downgrades are returned as account credit. Budget+ plans show there too: a cheaper one under **Downgrade**, a pricier one (with more RAM) under **Upgrade**. Your server moves to the Budget+ machines by itself, with your files, and the address changes. A server with Splits stays on Premium+ for now. See [Upgrading or downgrading](/billing/upgrades-and-downgrades).

## Sizes for each game

Every game uses this same table and price. Each game has a smallest and largest size that makes sense for it:

| Game | Premium+ sizes |
|---|---|
| Minecraft | 6 to 64 GB |
| Hytale | 6 to 32 GB |
| Palworld | 8 to 32 GB |
| FiveM | 6 to 16 GB |
| Terraria | 1 to 4 GB, see [Terraria plans](/plans/terraria-plans-and-prices) |

Discord bots have their own small plans, see [Discord bot hosting](/plans/discord-bot-hosting).

**Switching games:** a server can switch to any game whose smallest Premium+ size is at or below its plan, from **Settings > Change Game** on the panel. A smaller plan upgrades first and pays only the difference. See [Switching to a different game](/using_the_panel/basic-controls#switching-to-a-different-game).
