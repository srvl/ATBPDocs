## When a size is sold out

- Sold out means no new orders of that size for now; it is not gone. It opens again by itself when there is room.
- Ordering a sold-out size: billing lets you pick it, but checkout stops there. There is no **Pay** or checkout button and no invoice is made, so adding credit first does not help. Wait until the size is in stock again.
- Sizes mostly open again once a day, around 2 PM Philippine time (6:00 AM UTC), when servers that were not renewed are cleared. It is not guaranteed every day. There is no waitlist or reservation, so check billing around then.
- Want a ping when stock opens? On our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com), take the **Stocks** role in the self-roles channel. Staff ping that role when new stock is added, usually in #inquiries or #general.
- Asking about a size that is not listed, or a bigger setup? Open a Sales Inquiry ticket on Discord and say which size and game.
- Already have a server? Check the **Upgrade** page in billing even when a size shows sold out here; servers can sometimes still move up on the same machines when their machine has real room for them. See [Upgrading or downgrading](/billing/upgrades-and-downgrades).
- Promo codes that work right now are on [Current promotions](/billing/current-promotions).
