Terraria needs far less memory than most games, so it has its own small plans on both lines: Premium+ from 1 to 4 GB and Budget+ from 2 to 4 GB. They use the same price per GB as every other game (₱75 on Premium+, ₱35 on Budget+), just with smaller sizes. Servers are in Singapore. At checkout you choose vanilla, TShock or tModLoader.

| RAM | vCPU | Storage | Backups | Premium+ monthly | Budget+ monthly |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 GB | 1 | 8 GB | 3 | ₱75 | – |
| 2 GB | 1 | 16 GB (Budget+ 12 GB) | 3 | ₱150 | ₱70 |
| 3 GB | 1 | 24 GB (Budget+ 18 GB) | 3 | ₱225 | ₱105 |
| 4 GB | 1.5 (Budget+ 1) | 32 GB (Budget+ 24 GB) | 3 | ₱300 | ₱140 |

- Each server starts with 1 database and 5 ports (its main port plus 4 extra). Need more? Ask in a Sales Inquiry ticket and we'll raise it.
- Premium+ runs on AMD Ryzen 9 9950X, Budget+ on AMD EPYC 7B13. Premium+ has faster cores; Budget+ costs less than half as much for the same RAM.
- Longer terms are cheaper: 5% off for 3 months, 7.5% off for 6 months and 10% off for 12 months. There is no setup fee.
- 1 to 2 GB suits vanilla or a few friends; big modpacks in tModLoader do better on 3 or 4 GB.
- Upgrade or downgrade between Terraria sizes any time from billing (Services > your server > **Upgrade** or **Downgrade**). See [Upgrading or downgrading](/billing/upgrades-and-downgrades).
- Extra splits (₱20 each per month) and Extra Backups can be added like on any other plan.

**Switching games:** a Terraria plan can switch to another game only when the plan is at least that game's smallest size (Minecraft starts at 4 GB on Budget+ and 6 GB on Premium+). Upgrade first from billing and pay the difference, then pick the game in **Settings > Change Game**. Any bigger plan can switch to Terraria. See [Switching to a different game](/using_the_panel/basic-controls#switching-to-a-different-game).
