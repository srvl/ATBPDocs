title: "Build your first website"
description: "Three ways to put a website on ATBP web hosting: install WordPress, upload HTML files, or run a Node.js app. All from your billing service page."
---

Order a [web hosting plan](/plans/web-hosting), then open the service in billing. Everything below starts from that service page.

## Step 1: point your domain

Look at **Domain pointing** on your service page.

- If it says **Good**, go to step 2.
- If it says **Not yet**, it tells you exactly which A record to add at your domain provider. The value is `213.35.96.233`. A domain you bought from us is done for you.

Your site gets its free SSL certificate once the domain points. It is retried every hour, or press **Install SSL**.

## Step 2: pick how you want to build it

### WordPress, the easiest for most people

1. Press **Install WordPress**. It takes under a minute.
2. Your service page shows the address, user and password for 10 minutes. Save them.
3. Open the address and log in.

Install WordPress on a new website. If your website folder already has your own files, remove them first.

### Your own HTML files

1. Press **Open file manager**. It opens your website folder, `public_html`.
2. Upload your files. The first page must be named `index.html`. Delete the sample `index.html` that is already there.

To upload a whole folder, zip it, upload the zip, then right-click it and unzip it.

### A Node.js app

1. Press **Host a Node.js app**. A small sample app starts straight away.
2. Press **Open file manager**. It opens your app folder, `nodeapp`.
3. Upload your app and replace the sample `server.js`. Do not upload `node_modules`.
4. Press **Install Node.js packages** if you have a `package.json`, and wait a few minutes.
5. Press **Restart Node.js app**.

Your app must listen on the port we give it:

```js
app.listen(process.env.PORT);
```

We start it with the `start` script in your `package.json`. Without one, we look for `server.js`, `index.js`, `app.js` or `main.js`. **Node.js app output** on your service page shows its latest messages if something goes wrong.

Press **Switch back to a normal website** any time. Your `nodeapp` folder is kept.

## Databases and more websites

Press **Databases and more websites** on your service page. That page shows your domain and SSL status and has **Create a database** (the details show once, so save them), **Open phpMyAdmin** and **Add another website**, as far as your plan allows. **All tools** there opens the full control panel for scheduled tasks and backups.

## Uploading with an SFTP app

Apps like FileZilla or WinSCP are better for many files. Press **Generate new password**, then connect with:

- Protocol: SFTP
- Host: `web1.atbphosting.com`
- Port: `22`
- User: your panel user name, on your service page
- Password: the one you just generated
