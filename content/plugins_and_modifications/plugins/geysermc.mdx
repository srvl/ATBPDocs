### What does the plugin do?

GeyserMC enables clients from Minecraft Bedrock Edition to join your Minecraft Java server.  

## Usage

<Callout type="important">

GeyserMC requires your server to be on the latest version.

</Callout>

[Download the plugin](https://geysermc.org/download) and upload the jar into your `plugins` folder. Restart or turn on the server. If you need help installing plugins, check out [this guide](/plugins_and_modifications/installing-plugins).  

Once you've installed it, open the **Network** tab in the panel and add a port from the **Bedrock** range (see [Adding Ports](/using_the_panel/adding-ports)). Then open `plugins/Geyser-Spigot/config.yml` (or your Geyser config) and set `port` under `bedrock` to the port you were given.

After that, restart the server. Bedrock players join with your server's IP address and that port.  

<Callout type="important">

Players who are using the console version of Bedrock Minecraft must follow the instructions listed on [this page](https://wiki.geysermc.org/geyser/using-geyser-with-consoles/) as the console version does not allow joining of custom servers by default

</Callout>

<Callout type="info">

If you're also using Floodgate, you will have to set `auth-type` to `floodgate` in your Geyser config.yml

</Callout>
