---
title: "Find your Steam ID"
description: "Get the 17-digit Steam64 ID that game servers use for admins, bans and whitelists."
---

Many games identify players by their Steam ID: Rust, Valheim, 7 Days to Die, Project Zomboid, Palworld, DayZ and more. You need it to make someone admin, to ban them, or to add them to a whitelist.

The format game servers want is the **Steam64 ID**: a 17-digit number that usually starts with `7656`, for example `76561198000000000`. Older formats such as `STEAM_0:1:12345` or `[U:1:12345]` will not work in most server files.

## From the Steam app

1. In Steam, click your name at the top right and choose **Account details**.
2. Your Steam ID is shown under your account name. Copy the number.

## From your profile link

Open your Steam profile in a browser. If the address looks like `steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198000000000`, the number at the end is your Steam64 ID.

If it looks like `steamcommunity.com/id/yourname`, you have a custom profile link. Paste that link into a Steam ID lookup site such as [steamid.io](https://steamid.io) and copy the **steamID64** value. Do not remove your custom link to see the number; someone else could claim it.

## Another player's ID

Ask them to send it using the steps above, or paste their profile link into a lookup site. Many games also print the ID in the server **Console** when a player joins or tries to join, which is often the quickest way.
