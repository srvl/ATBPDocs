Hello! 👋

This guide covers where to change your account details, such as your name, email and password.

---

## Billing Account

Go to https://billing.atbphosting.com/account and log in (or register an account). From there you can edit your personal details, email and password. Save your changes when you are done.

---

## Game Panel Account

Log in to https://panel.atbphosting.com, then open your account page by clicking your name or avatar in the top right corner. Here you can change your email and password, and manage 2FA (see the [2FA guide](/using_the_panel/2fa)).

Your billing account and your game panel account are separate, so changing one does not change the other.
