This guide covers where to change your account details, such as your name, email and password.

---

## Billing Account

Go to https://billing.atbphosting.com/account and log in (or register an account). From there you can edit your personal details, email and password. Save your changes when you are done.

**Signing up with Google or Discord:** on the register page, choose **Sign up with Google or Discord**. After you confirm with Google or Discord, the form opens with your name and email filled in (and your Discord username, for Discord). Add your phone, country and address and press **Sign up**. There is no password to set: next time, sign in with the same Google or Discord button. If you want a password as well, use **Forgot password** on the login page. Changed your mind? Press **Use your email instead** on the register page to sign up with your email and a password.

**Country:** pick it from the list. If your account was made before the list existed and its country could not be matched, the account page asks you to pick it the next time you save.

**Phone number:**

- **In the Philippines:** enter your mobile number, for example `09171234567` or `+63 917 123 4567`. It is saved as 11 digits starting with `09`. If you pay with GCash or Maya, use the number you send from, so your payments are matched to your account automatically. If you top up at a GCash reloading station, the station's number may already be on another customer's account; that is allowed, but automatic top-ups from a number on more than one account wait for staff to match them, so use your own number when you can.
- **Outside the Philippines:** enter your own phone number with its country code, for example `+84 912 345 678`. Spaces and dashes are fine. You can pay with an exact-amount top-up from the Credits page or any other payment method.

## Game Panel Account

Log in to https://panel.atbphosting.com, then open your account page by clicking your name or avatar in the top right corner. Here you can change your email and password, and manage 2FA (see the [2FA guide](/using_the_panel/2fa)).

Your billing account and your game panel account are separate, so changing one does not change the other.

## Cannot log in or see your server

- The billing site and the panel have separate logins and separate passwords. "These credentials do not match" usually means the billing password was used on the panel, or the other way round.
- Your panel account is the email from the "Your server is ready" message. Use **Forgot password** on the panel to set a password.
- Signing up on the panel with a different method, such as Google, can create a new empty account with "no servers". Log in with the email your server was created under.
- Opening the panel from Discord's built-in browser often loses your login. Open the link in Chrome, Safari or another normal browser.
- Never post your password in a ticket. Staff will never ask for it.
- Helpers should not share your login. Add them as sub-users, see [Users](/using_the_panel/users).

## Linking Discord to billing

Run `/billing link` in your ticket or the bot commands channel on our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com), while you are logged in to https://billing.atbphosting.com in the same browser. Linking gives you the Client role and lets staff see your services when you open a ticket.

If it says the billing account is already linked, it is linked to another Discord account (often an old one). Open a ticket and staff will unlink it.
