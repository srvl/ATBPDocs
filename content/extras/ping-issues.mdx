---
title: "How to diagnose and fix ping issues"
description: "Run a traceroute or MTR to find where lag or packet loss starts, and what to send support."
---

Our servers are in Singapore. Players in the Philippines usually see low ping, so steady high ping, packet loss or random disconnects mean something along the route is struggling. These tests show where it starts: on your Wi-Fi, at your internet provider, somewhere in between, or at the server. Run one before opening a ticket and attach the result; it saves a lot of back and forth.

In every command below, use only your server's IP address, without the `:port` part.

## Quick check: traceroute

A traceroute lists every hop between you and the server and how long each one takes to answer.

- **Windows**: open Command Prompt and run `tracert 123.45.67.89`
- **macOS or Linux**: open Terminal and run `traceroute 123.45.67.89`

Replace the address with your server's IP. It takes a minute to finish.

## Better check: MTR

A traceroute is a single snapshot. MTR keeps pinging every hop, so it catches loss that comes and goes.

**Windows**

1. Download [WinMTR](https://sourceforge.net/projects/winmtr/), extract it and open `WinMTR.exe`.
2. Enter your server's IP in the **Host** field and press **Start**.
3. Let it run for at least a minute (100 packets or more), then press **Stop**.
4. Take a screenshot, or press **Copy Text to clipboard** and paste it in your ticket.

**Linux**: install `mtr` from your package manager and run `mtr 123.45.67.89`. **macOS**: install it with Homebrew (`brew install mtr`).

## Reading the result

- **Loss on one hop and every hop after it** points to a problem at that hop.
- **Loss on one hop only, with the hops after it at 0%** is usually fine. Many routers simply do not answer test packets.
- **Loss on your first hop or two** is your own router or Wi-Fi. Try a cable, restart the router, or move closer.
- **A big jump in time that stays high on later hops** is a slow or busy link at that point.
- Run the test two or three times, ideally while the lag is happening. One run can mislead.

## Sending it to us

Open a ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with:

- a full screenshot of the MTR or traceroute, not a summary
- the time the lag happened
- whether it happens all the time or only sometimes, and whether other players see it too

If only one player lags while everyone else is fine, the problem is almost always on that player's connection.

## Minecraft: Paper Anti-Xray

On Paper servers, Anti-Xray engine mode 2 can cause lag for players on unstable connections or on busy servers. Try engine mode 1, restart, and see if the lag goes away. The setting (`anti-xray`, `engine-mode`) is in `config/paper-world-defaults.yml`. See [Anti-Xray](/running_a_server/anti-xray).
