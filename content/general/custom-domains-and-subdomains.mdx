---
title: "Custom domains and free subdomains"
description: "Give your game server an easy address: a free ATBP subdomain or your own custom domain, and how to set either up."
---

- **Free subdomain:** in the panel go to **More > Subdomain** and click **Create subdomain**. It points at your server automatically. Free subdomains are a courtesy: a subdomain used for abuse, impersonation or anything against the Terms is removed.

  ![The panel's More > Subdomain page with the Create subdomain button and no subdomains yet](/screenshots/panel-more-subdomain.webp)

- **Naming:** on our shared domains a subdomain is one word of lowercase letters, numbers and dashes, like `mysmp` (no dots). Names that look like ATBP's own addresses are reserved, for example `panel`, `billing`, `support`, `login`, `pay` or anything with `atbp` in it. On a domain you bought, you can use any name, dots included.
- **How many:** up to 5 subdomains per server, and one subdomain per port. Delete one to free its slot.
- **Removing a port:** a subdomain on a port you remove from the Network tab is deleted within about 30 minutes, so it can never point at whoever gets that port next.
- **Pick the right port:** a Game Servers subdomain must use your server's **primary** port, the main one in the **Network** tab. On a Java server with Geyser that is the Java port, not the Bedrock port. If the subdomain does not connect but the numeric IP and port do, it was most likely made on the wrong port: delete it and create it again on the primary port, then wait a few minutes.
- **Subdomain type:** pick **Game Servers** for players to join your server. Pick **Web App** for anything that opens in a browser, such as a web map or dashboard, and choose that plugin's port; a Web App subdomain gives you an `https://` address. See [Web addresses (HTTPS)](/using_the_panel/ports-and-proxies).
- **Buying or transferring a domain:** search at https://billing.atbphosting.com/domains. See [Buying, transferring and managing domains](/general/domains) for the steps. Domains are sold for **one year** and are not renewed through us right now (see [Renewals](/general/domains#renewals)). A domain name cannot be changed after it is bought; a different spelling is a new domain.
- Domains are **not refundable** once registered.
- After buying, open a ticket with your server address (IP:port) so staff can set it up. If you bought a domain elsewhere, point it with an A record to your server IP and an SRV record (`_minecraft._tcp`) with your port, or move its nameservers to Cloudflare first.
- Bedrock players still need to enter the port unless your server uses the default Bedrock port.
