---
title: "Server optimisation"
---

- Basic optimisation help is **free** for all customers: we look at your Spark report, tell you what is lagging and which settings to change.
- In-depth, hands-on optimisation (for example reworking a world full of farms) is a **paid** service. Staff quote the price in your ticket before starting, and nothing is charged unless you agree.
- Start with the **Auto Optimizer** egg (panel > Settings > change server type). It tunes Paper/Leaf settings on every start. After turning it on (or choosing it), go to Settings > Reinstall Server so it applies; make a backup first. Note: it rewrites view and simulation distance each start; switch to the plain LeafMC egg if you want to set these yourself.
- For lag, open a Hosting Support ticket with a Spark report: run `/spark profiler --timeout 120`, then share the link. For lag spikes use `/spark profiler --only-ticks-over 100 --timeout 120`.
- Common fixes: lower view distance to 6 to 8, limit mob farms and entities, update plugins to versions made for your Minecraft version, and pre-generate your world with Chunky at night instead of during peak hours.
- MSPT under 50 is healthy. If you lag with only a few players, take the Spark report while the lag is happening and players are online.
- Staff will tell you what the Spark report shows. Fixing third-party plugins or custom code is best effort.
- Once we have optimised or cleaned your server, free or paid, the 3-day full refund no longer applies. See the [refund policy](/billing/refund-policy).
