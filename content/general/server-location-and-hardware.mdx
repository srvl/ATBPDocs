---
title: "Server location and hardware"
description: "Where ATBP game servers run (Singapore), the CPUs behind Budget+ and Premium+, and the ping to expect from the Philippines."
---

- **All servers are in Singapore.**
- **Premium+:** AMD Ryzen 9 9950X (up to 5.7 GHz), DDR5, storage in RAID 1.
- **Budget+:** AMD EPYC 7B13.
- **DDoS protection:** Budget+ and Premium+ servers are protected by TCPShield.
- There is no Philippines location. From Metro Manila, Singapore is usually 40 to 80 ms. Players in the US or Europe will see about 150 to 180 ms; a VPN or proxy will not lower that.
- vCPU is capped, not burstable: 1 vCPU = 100% CPU in the panel (4 vCPU shows 400%).
- CPU Guardian: a server that uses more CPU than its plan includes, even by a little, is throttled automatically to half its CPU. It is restored automatically. If it keeps happening, find the cause with [Spark](/running_a_server/spark) or upgrade vCPU.
- The panel shows which node your server is on under Settings. Whether each node is up is public at https://status.atbphosting.com, and https://atbphosting.com/network/ shows live whether lag comes from your ISP, our DDoS protection, the data centre or our servers.
- Moving a server to another node changes its IP (and sometimes ports). Ask in a ticket.
