---
title: "Server location and hardware"
---

- **All servers are in Singapore.**
- **Premium+:** AMD Ryzen 9 9950X (up to 5.7 GHz), DDR5, NVMe in RAID 1.
- **Budget+:** AMD EPYC 7B13.
- There is no Philippines location. From Metro Manila, Singapore is usually 40 to 80 ms. Players in the US or Europe will see about 150 to 180 ms; a VPN or proxy will not lower that.
- vCPU is capped, not burstable: 1 vCPU = 100% CPU in the panel.
- The panel shows which node your server is on under Settings. Node health is public at https://status.atbphosting.com.
- Moving a server to another node changes its IP (and sometimes ports). Ask in a ticket.
