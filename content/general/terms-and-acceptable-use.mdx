ATBP Hosting is run by ATBP Hosting Ltd. (Alberta, Canada). The full Terms of Service, last updated 23 October 2026, are at https://atbphosting.com/terms/. Key points:

- Every order asks you to tick two boxes in the cart, one for the Terms of Service and one for the [Refund Policy](/billing/refund-policy), before you can check out. We keep a record of what you agreed to with the order. The next order asks again.
- You must be 18 or older, or have a parent's or guardian's consent. One account per person or organisation.
- Services are prepaid. A new server is usually set up within a minute of the first payment.
- **Unpaid new orders:** an order that is not paid is cancelled by a daily check once it is more than 1 day old, so in practice 24 to 48 hours after you order (the check runs at 2:00 PM Philippine time). Nothing is charged. Billing emails you, and shows a notification in the billing area, about 1 hour after you order, and sends a last reminder about 19 hours after you order. Each email names the exact cancel time in Philippine time. Pay with GCash, Maya or bank transfer, or top up credits first and pay from them. You can turn each reminder off in your billing notification settings (**Remind me about orders waiting for payment** and **Remind me before an unpaid order is cancelled**). An unpaid order does not hold a slot: if the plan fills up before you pay, the invoice tells you there is no room and nothing is charged.
- **Ordering the same plan twice:** if you order the same plan with the same options (splits, extra backups, other options and the game you picked) while an order for it from the last 24 hours is still unpaid, billing opens that invoice with **You already have this order waiting for payment**, a **Pay this invoice** link and an **Order another one anyway** link. The server name does not matter. If you end up with two copies and pay one, the older unpaid copy (ordered up to 24 hours earlier) is cancelled by itself, unless you chose **Order another one anyway**.
- **Not allowed:** anything illegal, pirated content, DDoS or attack tools, network scanning or brute forcing, malware or phishing, spam, cryptocurrency mining, open proxies or VPN exit services, attacking other customers, or using so much CPU, disk or network that other customers suffer.
- **Proxies:** a Velocity, BungeeCord (or Waterfall and similar) or Gate proxy may only be hosted with us when the servers behind it are also hosted with us. Backends on any ATBP server count, including your own splits and servers on another ATBP plan or node. A proxy that sends players to another host uses a lot of bandwidth and is not allowed. The other way round is fine: a proxy hosted somewhere else may send players to servers hosted with us.
- **How proxies are checked:** we automatically read the backend addresses in your proxy's config (`velocity.toml` or `config.yml`) every 15 minutes. If every backend is hosted somewhere else on two checks in a row, the server owner gets an email naming the addresses and a deadline 48 hours later. If the proxy still points elsewhere after the deadline, the server is suspended. Pointing the proxy at servers hosted with us, or stopping the proxy, cancels the deadline by itself. A proxy with some backends here and some elsewhere is reviewed by staff instead. A suspended proxy stays suspended until you open a ticket in our [Discord server](https://discord.atbphosting.com); renewing does not lift it. If the proxy is the main server of a plan with splits, its splits are suspended with it.
- **Owner of a server:** the owner is the person whose billing account and panel account the server belongs to. Screenshots or messages are not proof of ownership. To transfer a server, the current owner opens a ticket, and the new owner first creates their own billing account. A server has one owner; share access with sub-users instead.
- Breaking these rules can mean immediate suspension, and termination without refund for serious or repeated cases.
- We may remove malicious code from files on your server and restart it while no players are online to do so. Files that cannot be cleaned safely may be removed. See [Plugin safety](/plugins_and_modifications/installing-plugins#plugin-safety).
- Once we have optimised your server or cleaned malicious code or miners from it, the 3-day full refund in the [Refund Policy](/billing/refund-policy) no longer applies to that service.
- Our uptime target is 99.9% a month (a target, not a guarantee). Incidents are posted on https://status.atbphosting.com.
- Price changes apply from your next renewal, with notice beforehand.
- Privacy: https://atbphosting.com/privacy/. Send data requests to privacy@atbphosting.com, a Discord ticket, or the billing chat; we answer within 30 days.
- We count visits to our website, docs and billing pages with our own visit counter on our own server. It sets no cookies and keeps no IP addresses; it only shows how many people visit, which pages they open and where they came from. No advertising trackers, and visit data is never shared or sold.
