Ask yourself three things: how many players are online at once (not max-players), Java or Bedrock, and how many plugins or mods.

## Budget+ or Premium+

Both lines are in Singapore, protected by TCPShield, with no setup fee. The difference is the CPU and the price:

- **Budget+:** AMD EPYC 7B13, ₱35 per GB of RAM, 4 to 64 GB. More RAM per peso, good for vanilla, light plugins and smaller groups. For example 4 GB is ₱140 and 8 GB is ₱280 a month. See [Budget+ plans and prices](/plans/budget-plus-plans-and-prices).
- **Premium+:** AMD Ryzen 9 9950X with DDR5, ₱75 per GB of RAM, 6 to 64 GB. Faster cores, the better pick for big modpacks, many plugins or 30+ players. For example 8 GB is ₱600 a month. See [Premium+ plans and prices](/plans/premium-plus-plans-and-prices).

Not sure? Start on Budget+ and move to Premium+ later from billing; your files come with you. See [Upgrading or downgrading](/billing/upgrades-and-downgrades).

## Minecraft

| Server | Suggested start |
|---|---|
| Vanilla or light plugins, up to ~10 players | Budget+ 4 to 6 GB |
| 10 to 25 players, 15+ plugins | Premium+ 8 GB (3 vCPU) |
| 20 to 40 players | Premium+ 12 GB (4 vCPU) |
| 40 to 80 players, or a light modpack | Premium+ 12 to 16 GB |
| Modpack of about 100 to 200 mods, a few players | Premium+ 8 to 10 GB |
| Heavy modpacks (200+ mods) | Premium+ 12 GB or more |
| Networks (proxy + lobby + games) | Premium+ 32 GB+ with free splits |

## Good to know

- The smallest Minecraft size is Budget+ 4 GB (2 vCPU), which suits a few friends. Premium+ starts at 6 GB (2 vCPU), a good start for a public Java server or a busy Bedrock world.
- One account can have as many servers as you want to buy. Each server is its own service with its own plan and invoice.
- vCPU, RAM and disk are not sold on their own. Each plan size comes with the vCPU and disk in its plan table, so to get more vCPU or disk, move to a bigger size (see [Upgrades and downgrades](/billing/upgrades-and-downgrades)).
- There is no fixed number of mods per plan. What counts is how heavy the mods are (tech, world generation and big content mods use the most) and how many players load chunks at once. Performance mods help, but do not count them as free.
- Lag is usually caused by plugins, entities or chunk generation before RAM. Pre-generate your world (Chunky) at night, not at peak hours.
