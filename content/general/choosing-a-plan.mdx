---
title: "Which plan and size do I need?"
---

Ask yourself three things: how many players are online at once (not max-players), Java or Bedrock, and how many plugins or mods.

| Server | Suggested start |
|---|---|
| Vanilla or light plugins, up to ~10 players | Budget+ 4 to 6 GB |
| 10 to 25 players, 15+ plugins | Premium+ 8 GB (3 vCPU) |
| 20 to 40 players | Premium+ 12 GB (4 vCPU) |
| 40 to 80 players, or a light modpack | Premium+ 12 to 16 GB |
| Modpack of about 100 to 200 mods, a few players | Premium+ 8 to 10 GB |
| Heavy modpacks (200+ mods) | Premium+ 12 GB or more |
| Networks (proxy + lobby + games) | Premium+ 32 GB+ with free splits |

For other games, start here (every size up to the cap is available, same price per GB as Minecraft):

| Game | Start at | Largest |
|---|---|---|
| Terraria | 1 to 2 GB ([Terraria plans](/plans/terraria-plans-and-prices)) | 4 GB |
| FiveM | 6 GB for QBCore or ESX roleplay | 16 GB |
| Hytale | 6 GB | 32 GB |
| Palworld | 8 GB | 32 GB |

- The smallest Minecraft size is Budget+ 4 GB (2 vCPU), which suits a few friends. Premium+ starts at 6 GB (2 vCPU), a good start for a public Java server or a busy Bedrock world.
- There is no fixed number of mods per plan. What counts is how heavy the mods are (tech, world generation and big content mods use the most) and how many players load chunks at once. Performance mods help, but do not count them as free.
- Lag is usually caused by plugins, entities or chunk generation before RAM. Pre-generate your world (Chunky) at night, not at peak hours.
- You can upgrade later from billing without losing your world.
