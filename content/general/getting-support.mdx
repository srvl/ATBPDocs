title: "How to get support"
description: "How to reach ATBP Hosting support: Discord tickets, live chat, Messenger and email, and what to include so we can help fast."
---

- **Open a Discord ticket.** This is how we prefer to help. Open the ticket panel in **#create-a-ticket** on our Discord and pick the type that fits. Fill in the form, including your panel link for server problems. Mari, our AI assistant, may answer first from these docs, in tickets and in our chat channels. Staff still read every ticket and take over when you need a person. Mari reads screenshots: send a clear picture of the error, console or panel page, up to three at a time, and Mari answers from what it shows. If the picture is too small or blurry to read, Mari leaves it for staff, so pasting the error text as well always helps. If Mari cannot answer a question in a public channel, it tells you to open a ticket instead of guessing.

  ![The Support Tickets panel in the create-a-ticket channel on Discord, with the Select a ticket type menu marked](/screenshots/discord-ticket-panel.webp)

  ![Discord ticket types: Hosting Support, Sales Inquiry, Server Renewal, Creator Partnership, General Inquiry and Server Optimization](/screenshots/discord-ticket-types.webp)

  - **Hosting Support**: server problems, crashes, connection, panel or network issues.
  - **Sales Inquiry**: billing, pricing and plan advice.
  - **Server Renewal**: renewing a server you already have. Only members with the Client role can open it, which you get by linking your Discord to billing with `/billing link` while you have an active service (a domain on its own does not count). If Discord says you lack the role, link your account first or pick **General Inquiry** instead.
  - **General Inquiry**: general questions, credits, top-ups and giveaway claims.
  - **Server Optimization**: ask for free optimization help for your server (there is no paid optimization).
  - **Creator Partnership**: partnership applications (1000+ followers).
- If Discord is not an option, use the **live chat** (the chat bubble at the bottom right of https://atbphosting.com, https://billing.atbphosting.com and the game panel at https://panel.atbphosting.com once you are logged in), message our **Facebook page** on Messenger, or **email** us.
  - Pick what you need help with before you start, so your chat reaches the right staff.
  - Leave your email if you can. If you close the page before we answer, the reply is emailed to you.
  - If you are logged in on billing or the game panel, staff can see your account, so you don't need to explain who you are. In the game panel they also see which server you have open, so start the chat from that server's page.
  - Mari, our AI assistant, answers first, from these docs, and links the page it used. In the game panel it answers for the game of the server you have open, so a Bedrock server gets Bedrock steps. Stock answers come straight from billing and match what checkout accepts. Under each answer, press **Yes, thanks** if it helped or **Talk to a person** if not. In the game panel the assistant also looks at the server you have open when your first message is about it, or when any message describes a problem such as lag, tick errors or crashes, and answers from what it finds, such as an error in the console or files left over from a software change. It does this only for your own servers and never for billing or account questions, and nothing on your server changes unless you say yes. There is also **Check my server**, which has the assistant check your server again. You can send screenshots in the chat too. If you start with just a hello, it asks what you need help with. You can also type **agent** at any time, and anything the assistant cannot answer goes straight to our staff. After a chat is passed to staff, the assistant can still answer new questions, but it stays out while staff are replying.
  - In the game panel chat, the assistant can also check a server that is not working. Type **check my server**, press **Check my server** under an answer, or just tell it what is wrong. It reads that server's status, console, logs, crash files and settings files, what was changed on the panel in the last week, its schedules, and the settings and details of a plugin the logs blame, with passwords, keys, addresses and account names hidden, then tells you what it found. If it can fix the problem, for example a wrong setting or a server that needs a newer Java version, it shows the exact change and asks first: press **Fix it for me** to let it make the change, or **I'll fix it myself** (typing **yes** or **no** works too). You can ask about the change first, for example "will I lose my world?": it answers and the offer stays open. Nothing is changed without a yes; if your reply is unclear it asks once more, then passes the chat to our staff. After a fix that starts the server, it watches the server for up to about a minute and tells you whether it came online or stopped again. If the server stops at a question in the console, such as a world upgrade, it tells you what the question says and never answers it for you. If you only want to know what is wrong, say so, for example "just check it" or "teach me how", and you get the steps without a change on offer. It keeps a copy of every file it changes, never deletes anything, and **Undo** (or replying **undo**) puts things back; if the fix had started your server, it restarts it so the old settings take effect. If you uploaded a Bedrock world as a `.mcworld` file and do not know how to unpack it, tell the chat (for example "I uploaded my world"): on a Bedrock server it offers to unpack it into the `worlds` folder, set `level-name` and start the server, and your old world stays as it was. On a Java server it tells you that a `.mcworld` file is a Bedrock world. After a check, **Check my server again** runs it again, which is handy once you have fixed something yourself. It only checks servers you own, and only while you are logged in to the game panel. The result is written by AI and can be wrong, so read it before you say yes. If you have more than one server, start the chat from the page of the server you mean: it checks that one and says which server it picked, so you can tell it if you meant another. Otherwise it shows your servers as buttons to pick from. If the server is still starting, it watches it for up to about a minute until it is up or stops, and it also reads the log of a start that just failed. A check can take a minute or two. Within a few seconds you get "I am checking your server now", then the result in the same chat; there is no need to write again while you wait: other messages are set aside until the result comes, and typing **agent** still passes the chat to staff. If a whitelist keeps a friend out, it may tell you to type `whitelist add <player name>` in the console, with your friend's name in place of `<player name>`.
  - In the game panel chat, the assistant can also do things on your server for you. Ask in plain words, for example "restart my server", "install LuckPerms", "op Steve", "make a backup" or "switch my server to Paper". It can handle power and the console, files, backups, schedules, ports, databases and startup settings, plugins, mods and modpacks, the server version and game type, players (op, whitelist, bans and kicks), splits, and the Bedrock, FiveM, Hytale, Palworld and Project Zomboid tools in the panel. It can also answer questions about your server, such as which version it runs. Before it changes anything it says exactly what it will do and waits: press **Yes, do it** or **No** (typing **yes** or **no** works too). Before a reinstall, a version or game type switch, a backup restore, a modpack install or deleting files, it makes a backup first so the change can be undone from the **Backups** tab; if that backup cannot be made, nothing is changed. A player name, an email for a subuser, a download link or a command that gives someone control of your server is only used if you typed it yourself in the chat, and settings that decide who can join, such as `online-mode`, the whitelist or RCON, can only be changed to the safer side. It only works on servers you own, and only while you are logged in to the game panel. It is AI and can misread what you want, so read what it says it will do before you say yes. Our staff can also ask the assistant to make a change on your server from your chat's ticket; what it does then is shown to staff only, and it still only works on the servers you own.
  - For lag, the assistant works from a Spark report. Paste the link if you have one. If you don't and your Java server has Spark (Paper, Purpur and Leaf on 1.21 or newer include it), the assistant runs Spark itself for 20 seconds while it checks your server, reads the report and tells you what is lagging, such as a plugin, a Skript or entities. Spark only measures; nothing on your server changes. If Spark is not on your server, reply **install spark** and the assistant adds it after you say yes, or make a report yourself: run `spark profiler start --timeout 300` in the console while the lag is happening, wait 5 minutes, and paste the link that appears. Without a report it will not guess.
  - Billing Balance and Server Renewal chats go straight to our staff.
  - Every chat also opens a ticket for our staff, so you get the same help as a Discord ticket.
  - On Messenger, staff can only reply within 24 hours of your last message, so reply to us there if you are still waiting.
- Please don't DM staff on Discord or their personal accounts; those messages are not tracked and may be missed. Messages to our Facebook page are tracked.
- **Some problems can only be fixed by the owner or co-owner.** That covers certain billing and setup problems, for example a paid server that was not created. Other staff can see your ticket but cannot fix these themselves, so the fix can wait until the owner or co-owner is online, sometimes a few hours. Keep the ticket open; there is no need to open another one.
- Link your Discord to billing with `/billing link` first so staff can see your services.
- For lag, include a Spark report: run `/spark profiler start --timeout 120` and share the link.
- We support the hosting itself. Help with third-party plugins, mods and server design is best effort.

## Is ATBP down?

- **Check https://status.atbphosting.com first.** It shows whether each node is up, any incident or planned maintenance with staff updates until it is fixed, and, live, whether lag or join problems come from your ISP (PLDT, Globe, Converge, Smart, DITO), our DDoS protection, the data centre or our servers. Staff also post outages in the Discord announcements channel.
- **Signed in to billing?** The status card in the billing menu shows any open incident and planned maintenance starting within a day, with its latest update, and turns red during a major incident. Click it for the full details.
- **If the game panel won't load, your server is most likely still running.** Game servers run on their own machines, so players can usually keep playing while the panel is down; you just can't use the console or files until it is back.
- **If only your server is unreachable**, check its console in the game panel first; most of the time the server crashed or is still starting. If the console looks fine and nothing is announced, open a **Hosting Support** ticket with your panel link.
- Don't reinstall your server or delete files while you wait. An outage does not delete your data.
