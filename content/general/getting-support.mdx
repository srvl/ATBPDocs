---
title: "How to get support"
---

- **Open a Discord ticket.** This is how we prefer to help. Open the ticket panel in our Discord and pick Hosting Support, Sales Inquiry, Server Renewal or Billing. Fill in the form, including your panel link for server problems.
- If Discord is not an option, use the **live chat** (the chat bubble at the bottom right of https://atbphosting.com and https://billing.atbphosting.com), or **email** us.
  - Our AI assistant answers first. Type **agent** at any time to reach a person.
  - Every chat also opens a ticket for our staff, so you get the same help as a Discord ticket. Staff replies come back in the chat; keep the page open or come back to the same browser to see them.
- Please don't DM staff; DMs are not tracked and may be missed.
- Tickets close automatically after 12 hours with no reply; open a new one if you still need help.
- Link your Discord to billing with `/billing link` first so staff can see your services.
- For lag, include a Spark report: run `/spark profiler --timeout 120` and share the link.
- We support the hosting itself. Help with third-party plugins, mods and server design is best effort.
