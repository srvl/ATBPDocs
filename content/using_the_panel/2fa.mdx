This page shows you how to set up 2FA on your account. Only set up 2FA on devices you trust, never on a shared or compromised device.

<Callout type="warning">

Save the recovery codes shown when you set up 2FA. Without them you cannot get back into your account if you lose your verification device. Recovery codes from before the [new panel](/using_the_panel/new-panel) (November 2026) no longer work: if you had 2FA on then, save new ones from your account page.

</Callout>

## Game panel 2FA

Set up 2FA from your account page in the [game panel](https://panel.atbphosting.com).

1. Download a 2FA app on your phone, such as [Authy](https://authy.com/).
2. On your account page, turn on two-factor authentication to show a QR code.
3. Add a new account in the app and scan the QR code, then type the code the app shows into the panel.
4. Save the recovery codes the panel gives you somewhere safe, away from your phone.

You are set and fully protected! Remember to keep the device near you when you log in so you can authenticate yourself.

With 2FA on, signing in with Google or Discord on the panel login page asks for your 2FA code too.

## Billing 2FA

Your billing account has its own 2FA, separate from the panel's.

1. Log in at [billing.atbphosting.com](https://billing.atbphosting.com) and open **Account > Security** ([billing.atbphosting.com/account/security](https://billing.atbphosting.com/account/security)).
2. Under **Two-factor authentication**, press **Enable two-factor authentication**.
3. Scan the QR code with your 2FA app and enter the code it shows.
