---
title: "New game panel: what changed"
description: "ATBP moved to a new game panel on 21 November 2026 (PHT). What carried over, what you need to set up again, and what works differently."
---

On 21 November 2026 (PHT) the game panel moved to a new, faster panel with the ATBP look. It is at the same address, https://panel.atbphosting.com, and your game servers kept running through the switch.

## What carried over

- Your login: the **same email and password**.
- Your servers, files, backups, subdomains, sub-users, schedules and share links.
- 2FA: if it was on, it is still on, and your 2FA app keeps giving the right codes.
- Google, Discord and billing sign-in on the panel login page.
- The ATBP tools you used before, such as the billing card, changing your game, subdomains, Bedrock and crossplay settings, CPU Guardian and Free RAM.

## What you need to set up again

- **2FA recovery codes.** Old recovery codes do not work on the new panel. Open your account page and save the new ones somewhere safe. See [2-Factor Authentication](/using_the_panel/2fa).
- **API keys.** Old API keys, both client and application keys, did not carry over. If a bot, script or tool used one, create a new key on your account page and put it in that tool.
- **Google or Discord sign-in.** A Google or Discord login is never joined to an existing panel account by matching the email. To sign in with Google or Discord, log in with your email and password first and link Google or Discord from your account page. If you skip this, the Google or Discord button does not open your existing account and can leave you in an empty one with no servers.

## What works differently

- **Signed out once.** The switch signed everyone out, so log in again with your same email and password. If the panel says the password is wrong, press **Forgot password** on the game panel login page (not billing) and open the reset link in your email. See [Editing Account Details](/extras/account-details).
- **2FA with Google or Discord sign-in.** With 2FA on, signing in with Google or Discord now asks for your 2FA code before it lets you in.
- **Database backups.** We copy every MySQL database each night and keep 7 copies, which you can download or restore from the **Databases** tab. Copies made on the old panel did not carry over; the first ones on the new panel were made on the night of 21 November 2026. See [Databases](/using_the_panel/databases#database-backups).
- **Backups from before the switch.** They still restore and download as before, but you cannot open them with **Browse files** to pick single files. Restore the whole backup, or download it and take what you need. New backups can be browsed. See [Managing Backups](/using_the_panel/backups/guide).
- **Stop before a backup.** The option to stop the server before a backup is now its own button, **Stop and back up**, next to **Create Backup**. See [Managing Backups](/using_the_panel/backups/guide#stop-the-server-first).
- **Schedule history.** Past runs from the old panel did not carry over. The **Schedules** tab shows a run history again for runs since the switch. See [Schedules](/using_the_panel/schedules#run-history).
- **Activity.** Your server's and account's **Activity** tabs also show what happened on the old panel, back to August 2026.
- **Trash bin.** Files that were in the old file manager's trash bin before the switch are not shown in the new panel.
- **SFTP details.** They are in the **SFTP details** box on the **Settings** page, as before, and also under **Files** > **Connect** > **via SFTP**. Copy them into your SFTP app again, because the port can differ from the old panel's. Your SFTP password is still your game panel password. See [Using SFTP](/using_the_panel/sftp).
- **Main port.** A few servers came over with a main port outside their game's range, for example a Minecraft Java server whose port is not 25600 or higher. Players then get **Connection timed out** or cannot connect at all. Open the **Network** tab: if your Java server's **primary** port is below 25600, add a **Minecraft Java** port and click **Make Primary** on it, or open a ticket and we will move it for you. See [Adding Ports](/using_the_panel/adding-ports).

Something looks wrong on your server after the switch? Open a [support ticket](/general/getting-support) with your panel link.
