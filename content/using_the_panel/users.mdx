## What are subusers?

Subusers let other people control your server with the permissions you choose, without using your own account.

<Callout type="error">

Only give access to people you trust, and never give away your account's credentials. You could be held liable for any actions that break our Terms of Service.

</Callout>

The email you add must already be registered on [panel.atbphosting.com](https://panel.atbphosting.com).

### Adding the subusers

1.  Go to the server that you'd like to share access to.
2.  Open the **Users** section in the left sidebar. It is the 5th item down.
3.  Click on **New User** to start configuring access, enter their ATBP Hosting email and then select the permissions you'd like.
4.  Scroll to the very bottom of the users tab and click **Invite User**.

The person you added will get an email telling them you have shared your server with them.

The subuser will only see the server, and only the tabs, that match the permissions you granted.
