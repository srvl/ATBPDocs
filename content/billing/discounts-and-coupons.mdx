---
title: "Discounts, coupons and promo codes"
---

- **All coupons and promo codes are paused.** Old codes (for example 25OFF, LESS50, OCT40 or creator codes) no longer work.
- The only discounts are for longer billing terms: **5% off for 3 months, 10% off for 6 months, 15% off for 12 months**, on both Budget+ and Premium+.
- The same term discounts apply when you renew in advance with the **Renew** button (see [renewals](/billing/renewals-suspension-and-deletion)), so prepaying 12 months costs the same as buying a 12-month plan.
- When a code is running, it can be entered at checkout or on the Renew page. A code comes off the price after the term discount.
- Any future promotion will be announced in the Discord announcements channel.
