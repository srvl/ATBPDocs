---
title: "Discounts, coupons and promo codes"
---

- **Codes that work right now** are listed on [Current promotions](/billing/current-promotions), which updates by itself, and show in a banner at the top of atbphosting.com and billing. No code listed means no public promotion is running.
- Old codes (for example 25OFF, LESS50, OCT40 or creator codes) no longer work.
- The discounts for longer billing terms always apply: **5% off for 6 months and 10% off for 12 months** on every plan (Budget+, Premium+, Terraria and Discord bots) and on paid Splits and Extra Backups. 3 months is the monthly price times three.
- The same term discounts apply when you renew in advance with the **Renew** button (see [renewals](/billing/renewals-suspension-and-deletion)), so prepaying 12 months costs the same as buying a 12-month plan.
- Enter a code at checkout or on the Renew page. A code comes off the price after the term discount.
- Promo codes do not work on upgrades or downgrades, or on domains.
- New promotions are also posted in the Discord announcements channel.
