---
title: "How to change your account email address"
description: "How to change the email on your billing account and panel account."
---


Your billing account and your panel account are separate, so the email is changed in each place.

## Billing account

1. Log in at [billing.atbphosting.com](https://billing.atbphosting.com).
2. Open your [account page](https://billing.atbphosting.com/account).
3. Enter the new email address and save.

![The billing Account page with the Email box and the Update button marked](/screenshots/billing-account.webp)


## Panel account

1. Log in at [panel.atbphosting.com](https://panel.atbphosting.com).
2. Open the **Account** page.
3. Change the email address and save.

Use an email you can access. Server and invoice emails go there, and sometimes land in spam. If you lost access to your old email and cannot log in, open a ticket in our [Discord server](https://discord.atbphosting.com).

## Extra billing emails

If someone else pays for you, or you use another email when you talk to staff, add it under **Account > Billing emails** at [billing.atbphosting.com/account/billing-emails](https://billing.atbphosting.com/account/billing-emails). Staff can then find your account by that email too. Your login email does not change.

1. Enter the email and press **Add**. You can add up to 3.
2. We send a link to that email. It counts once its owner clicks the link (the link works for 48 hours; use **Send again** if it ran out).
3. Remove an email any time with **Remove**.
