description: "How to change the email on your billing account and panel account."
---

import { Callout } from 'nextra/components'

Your billing account and your panel account are separate, so the email is changed in each place.

## Billing account

1. Log in at [billing.atbphosting.com](https://billing.atbphosting.com).
2. Open your [account page](https://billing.atbphosting.com/account).
3. Enter the new email address and save.

## Panel account

1. Log in at [panel.atbphosting.com](https://panel.atbphosting.com).
2. Open the **Account** page.
3. Change the email address and save.

<Callout type="warning">

Use an email you can access. Server and invoice emails go there, and sometimes land in spam. If you lost access to your old email and cannot log in, open a ticket in our [Discord server](https://discord.atbphosting.com).

</Callout>
