---
title: "Refund policy"
---

- **Within 3 days of purchase:** you can ask for a full refund, as long as the Terms of Service were not broken.
- **No free trials:** we don't give trial or test servers, not even for a few hours. The 3-day full refund is there so you can try a server and get your money back if you are not happy.
- **After 3 days:** refunds are generally not given. Only in special circumstances do we make an exception, decided case by case. Cancelling part way through a term normally does not refund the remaining days.
- **Used our optimisation or cleanup?** If our staff or our automated tools optimised your server, or removed malware, backdoors or crypto miners from it, the 3-day full refund no longer applies. That includes moving the optimised or cleaned server to another host. Any refund is then case by case and may be reduced or declined.
- **Not refundable:** services suspended for breaking the Terms, account credit you added as prepayment, promotional or goodwill credit, and domains once registered.
- **Paid twice or paid the wrong account?** Tell us with the payment reference and we will sort it out.
- **Server could not be set up:** your payment is returned as account credit automatically.
- Downgrades are returned as account credit, not cash.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method where possible, otherwise as account credit.
- **How to ask:** in billing open Services, pick the server and press **Refund**. See [how to request a refund](/billing/refunds).
