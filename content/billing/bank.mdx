---
title: "Paying by bank transfer"
---

Any Philippine bank that can send money to GCash over **InstaPay** can be used. No GCash account of your own is needed. Unless **Bank transfer** shows a yellow **Manual** tag, your payment is matched and credited automatically.

## Paying an invoice

1. Open the invoice on https://billing.atbphosting.com and choose **Bank transfer**.
2. Enter the **exact amount** you will send and the **last 4 digits of the bank account number** you send from.
3. Send exactly that amount by InstaPay to the GCash number shown.
4. The payment is matched by the amount and the last 4 digits, and the invoice is paid automatically.

## Adding credit

On the [Credits page](https://billing.atbphosting.com/account/credits), choose **Bank transfer** and follow the same steps. The credit is added to your balance.

> [!WARNING]
> **Last 4 digits means the account number, not your card.** Use the last 4 digits of the bank account number you send from, as shown in your bank app. Do not use the number on your ATM or debit card; a card number cannot be matched.

## When Bank transfer shows Manual

If Bank transfer shows the yellow **Manual** tag, staff confirm it instead. After sending, open a Billing ticket on [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with your receipt and your account email.

If your payment does not show up after a few minutes, open a Billing ticket with your receipt.
