---
title: "Paying by bank transfer"
description: "Pay an invoice or add credit by InstaPay bank transfer to our Maya or GCash."
---

Any Philippine bank app that can send money over **InstaPay** can be used, to our **Maya** or our **GCash**. You do not need a Maya or GCash account of your own. A destination without a yellow **Manual** tag is matched and credited automatically.

Use InstaPay, not PESONet. PESONet transfers arrive hours later, after the time to match them has run out.

## Paying an invoice

1. Open the invoice on https://billing.atbphosting.com and choose **Bank transfer**.
2. Under **Send by InstaPay to**, choose **Maya** or **GCash**.
3. **To Maya:** enter how many pesos you will send (the amount due is filled in). You get the exact amount to send and a 15-minute timer. Usually it is just the amount you chose, for example **₱150.00**; a few centavos are added (**₱150.37**) only if someone else is paying the same amount at that moment. Send exactly that amount by InstaPay to the Maya number shown before the timer ends.
4. **To GCash:** enter the exact amount you will send and the **last 4 digits of the bank account number** you send from, then send exactly that amount by InstaPay to the GCash number shown.
5. The invoice is paid automatically once the transfer is matched, usually within a minute.

If you send a little more than the invoice, the rest stays in your balance as credit.

## Adding credit

On the [Credits page](https://billing.atbphosting.com/account/credits), choose **Bank transfer**, then **To Maya** or **To GCash**, and follow the same steps. The credit is added to your balance.

> [!WARNING]
> **For GCash, the last 4 digits are the account number, not your card.** Use the last 4 digits of the bank account number you send from, as shown in your bank app. A number from your ATM or debit card cannot be matched. Sending to Maya does not need these digits; the exact amount is enough.

## When a destination shows Manual

Maya or GCash can show a yellow **Manual** tag inside Bank transfer, for example while that wallet's notifications are delayed. Then staff confirm it instead: send by InstaPay, and open a **General Inquiry** ticket on [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with your receipt and your account email. If the other destination shows no tag, you can use it for an automatic payment.

If your payment does not show up after a few minutes, open a **General Inquiry** ticket with your receipt.

## Paid but the invoice still says Waiting for payment

1. Link your Discord to your billing account: run `/billing link` in our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) and follow the steps. Payments can only be matched to a linked account. A billing account can be linked to one Discord account at a time; if you see "already linked", ask staff to unlink the old one.
2. Open a **General Inquiry** ticket with a clear screenshot of the receipt (reference number, amount and time) and your account email.
3. Staff add the amount to your account credit. When Manual crediting is on, this depends on staff availability and can take several hours, longer overnight (Philippine time).
4. When staff say the credit is added, open the invoice and choose **Pay with credits**. Credit in your balance does not pay the invoice by itself, so the invoice (and a suspended server) stays unpaid until you do.

Sent the wrong amount, after the timer ran out, or twice? Tell staff in the ticket. Extra money stays as credit, or a duplicate payment is returned to the original method.
