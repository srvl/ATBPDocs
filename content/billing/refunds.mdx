This guide shows how to ask for a refund. For the rules on who qualifies, read the [refund policy](/billing/refund-policy). The live policy is also at [atbphosting.com](https://atbphosting.com/refund-policy).

## Who can get a refund

- **Within 3 days of purchase:** a full refund of everything you paid, as long as the Terms of Service were not broken. No fee is kept back, and there is no setup fee.
- **After 3 days:** refunds are generally not given; only in special circumstances, decided case by case.
- **After optimisation or cleanup:** if we optimised your server or removed malware, backdoors or crypto miners from it, the 3-day full refund no longer applies and any refund is case by case.
- **Unspent credit you topped up:** not normally refunded, but as a case by case exception we may refund it when we can trace it to a real cash payment. See the [refund policy](/billing/refund-policy) for how to ask.

## How to request a refund

1. Download a backup of your files from the panel (see the [backups guide](/using_the_panel/backups/guide)).
2. On https://billing.atbphosting.com open **Services**, pick the server and press **Refund**.

   ![Billing service page: the Refund button at the top right, next to Renew, Cancel service, Upgrade and Downgrade](/screenshots/billing-service-refund-button.webp)

3. The **Request a refund** page first shows what you would get back: whether you are inside the 3-day refund period, the amount, what you paid and how many days ago you ordered. After the 3 days it says the period has passed, but you can still ask and we will look at it.
4. Fill in the one form:
   - **Where should the money go?** Pick **The way I paid** (back to the account or wallet you paid from; enter the account number and name under **Account details**) or **My account balance** (credit for any order or renewal).
   - **Why do you want a refund?** A short reason, up to 500 characters.

   Then press **Send request**.

   ![The refund form: Where should the money go? with The way I paid picked and My account balance next to it, Account details, Why do you want a refund? and the Send request button](/screenshots/billing-refund-page.webp)

5. **What happens next**, as the page shows it: we look at your request and reply by email; your server keeps running until the refund is approved; once it is approved, the money is sent and the server is removed. The page then says when you asked, and earlier requests are listed with their status.

No Refund button, or it says the service cannot be refunded right now? Open a **Billing** ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with your invoice number and billing email.

<Callout type="warning">

Once a refund is approved or a cancellation is processed, your server and its files are deleted and cannot be recovered. Download a backup first.

</Callout>

Refunds go back to the original payment method where possible, otherwise as account credit.
