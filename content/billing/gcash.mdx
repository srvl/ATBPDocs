---
title: "Paying with GCash"
description: "Pay for your ATBP game server with GCash: how to send, how top-ups are added to your account, and what to do if a payment is not credited."
---

GCash top-ups are added to your account automatically, unless GCash shows a yellow **Manual** tag on the [Credits page](https://billing.atbphosting.com/account/credits). There are two ways to send.

## Option 1: Send from your saved number

1. On the [Credits page](https://billing.atbphosting.com/account/credits), open the **GCash** tab and enter the number you will send from under **You send from**. It saves as you type.
2. Send any amount to the GCash number shown on that page. 1 PHP = 1 credit, so send ₱550 for 550 credits.
3. Your credit is added within about a minute. The page updates on its own.

> [!WARNING]
> This option only works when you send from the **same number saved on your account**. Sending from another number? Use option 2.

## Option 2: Send an exact amount from any GCash

1. On the Credits page, open **Sending from another number?**, enter how many pesos to add and press **Get exact amount**.
2. You get an amount with extra centavos, for example **₱500.37**, and a 15-minute timer.
3. Send **exactly** that amount, centavos included, to the GCash number or QR code shown, before the timer ends. It can come from any GCash account.
4. Your credit is added within about a minute.

## When GCash shows Manual

If GCash shows the yellow **Manual** tag, automatic crediting is paused and exact-amount top-ups are not offered. GCash can switch to Manual by itself when automatic top-ups stop going through, and back to Auto when they work again, so the tag may change within a few minutes. You can still pay to the same number, then upload your receipt. No ticket is needed.

1. Pay with GCash to the number or QR code shown.

   ![Credits page, GCash tab: the GCash number to send to and the Show QR code link](/screenshots/credits-gcash-number.webp)

2. Take a screenshot of the GCash receipt, the screen that shows the **Ref No.**, amount and time. A clear photo of that screen taken with another phone works too.
3. Upload it:

   ![Credits page when GCash shows Manual: enter the amount you sent and press Choose picture to upload your GCash receipt](/screenshots/credits-gcash-upload-receipt.webp)

   - for an invoice: open the invoice, pay with **GCash**, confirm your number and press **I've sent it**. On the **Upload** step, check that the amount matches this receipt and choose the picture;
   - for a top-up: on the [Credits page](https://billing.atbphosting.com/account/credits), open the **GCash** tab, type the amount you sent and upload the screenshot.
4. Staff check the receipt. An accepted receipt goes to your balance. On an invoice, tap **Pay now** once your balance covers it. The **Status** step lists each receipt with its state, and tapping one shows the details. If a receipt is not accepted, you get an email with the reason and can upload the right one.

**Sent less than the invoice?** Upload that receipt, send the rest, then upload the second receipt on the same invoice. You can have up to 5 receipts waiting on one invoice. The **Status** step shows what the invoice still needs, what is on your balance and what is left to send. If you open the invoice again later, it starts on this step, and **Add receipt** takes you back to the upload. Once your balance covers the invoice, **Pay now** appears: tap it to pay, and a suspended server starts again. Anything you sent above the total stays on your balance.

Upload the screenshot or photo straight from your phone as a JPG, PNG or WebP picture. A screenshot is best. A big camera photo is made smaller on your phone before it is sent and turned upright for you, so it goes through quickly even on mobile data; the whole receipt must still be in it and readable. Do not crop out the reference number or edit the picture.

While it uploads, a bar shows how far along it is. If your signal drops, it tries again by itself, and a receipt is never saved twice. If it still cannot get through, you see a message: check your Wi-Fi or mobile data and tap **Upload receipt** again. If the page says it expired, reload it and upload again. iPhone photos saved as HEIC that the browser cannot convert are refused with a message; take a screenshot of the receipt and upload that instead.

Each receipt, and each reference number, is credited once, so a receipt already used is not accepted again. You can send up to 5 receipts an hour; a picture that is refused does not count toward that.

If the phone's GCash notification for your payment comes in later, it is matched to your receipt and not added twice.

If you cannot upload, or a payment takes longer than a few hours, open a **General Inquiry** ticket on [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with your receipt.

## Paid but the invoice still says Waiting for payment

1. Link your Discord to your billing account: run `/billing link` in our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) and follow the steps. Payments can only be matched to a linked account. A billing account can be linked to one Discord account at a time; if you see "already linked", ask staff to unlink the old one.
2. When GCash shows **Manual**, upload the receipt on the invoice's payment screen (see [When GCash shows Manual](#when-gcash-shows-manual)). Otherwise, or if you cannot upload, open a **General Inquiry** ticket with a clear screenshot of the receipt (reference number, amount and time) and your account email.
3. Staff add the amount to your account credit. When Manual crediting is on, this depends on staff availability and can take several hours, longer overnight (Philippine time).
4. Credit from an approved receipt or from a ticket stays on your balance until you pay with it: open the invoice, click **Account Credits** under **Pay with Credits**, then **Apply Credits and Pay**. An open invoice page updates by itself, so you do not need to refresh it.

Sent the wrong amount, from another number, or twice? Tell staff in the ticket. Extra money stays as credit, or a duplicate payment is returned to the original method.
