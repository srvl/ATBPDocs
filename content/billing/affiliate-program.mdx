---
title: "Affiliate program"
description: "How the ATBP Hosting affiliate program works: where to find your own link in billing and how you earn account credit from it."
---

You can earn account credit by sharing ATBP Hosting with friends, your community or your viewers.

1. Log in to [billing](https://billing.atbphosting.com) and open **Account > Affiliate**.
2. Copy your own affiliate link from that page and share it.
3. When someone orders through your link, you earn credit on your ATBP account. The page shows the current commission rate and what you have earned so far.

- Your earnings are **account credit**, which pays your own invoices the same way as credit you add yourself. See [Payment methods and account credit](/billing/payment-methods).
- Creator partners have their own codes and discounts; see [Discounts, coupons and promo codes](/billing/discounts-and-coupons).
- Questions about the program or an earning that does not show? Open a ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com).
