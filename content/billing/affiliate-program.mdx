You can earn commission by sharing ATBP Hosting with friends, your community or your viewers.

1. Log in to [billing](https://billing.atbphosting.com) and open **Account > Affiliate**.
2. Press **Copy** on the **Your affiliate link** card and share the link. The card also counts **Visitors** and **Signups** from your link.
3. When someone orders through your link and pays, you earn **10% of their first payment** for that order. Renewals of that order do not earn again.

## When you can use it

- A new commission is **on hold for 14 days** after the payment. After that it shows as **Available**.
- Your very first commission, and any single commission over ₱500, is also checked by staff before it becomes available. It shows as **Being checked** until then. If staff do not approve it, it shows as **Reversed** with the reason.
- If the order is refunded or the payment is disputed during the hold, that commission is cancelled. If it is refunded after you already used the commission, the amount is taken from your next commissions.
- Available commission that you do not use **expires 12 months** after it became available. The Affiliate page shows how much expires in the next 30 days.

## Using your commission

The top of the Affiliate page shows **Commission you can use now**, with the amount still on hold under it and any amount that expires within 30 days. Once you have commission available, the two ways to use it show side by side:

- **Add to my credit**: any amount, instantly. Enter the amount and press **Add**. Credit pays your own renewals and new orders. See [Payment methods and account credit](/billing/payment-methods).
- **Cash out to GCash or Maya** (when cash payouts are open; the Affiliate page only shows this option then): from **₱500**, up to **₱5,000 per month** (requests still waiting count toward the month). Press **Cash out**, pick GCash or Maya, enter the amount, your number and the name on the account, then press **Request payout**. Staff send it within a few days.

**Payouts** lists your latest 10 cash-out requests with their status: **Waiting**, **Being sent**, **Sent**, **Declined** (with the reason) or **Cancelled**. While a request says **Waiting**, you can press **Cancel** on it; once staff start sending it, it can no longer be cancelled.

**Commission** shows what you earned in each of the last 12 months and your latest 30 commissions with their status (**On hold**, **Being checked**, **Available**, **Used**, **Reversed** or **Expired**). **CSV** downloads the full list. The **How commission works** box sums up the rules on this page.

- Creator partners have their own codes and discounts; see [Discounts, coupons and promo codes](/billing/discounts-and-coupons).
- Questions about the program or a commission that does not show? Open a ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com).
