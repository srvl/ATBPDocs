You can earn commission by sharing ATBP Hosting with friends, your community or your viewers.

1. Log in to [billing](https://billing.atbphosting.com) and open **Account > Affiliate**.
2. Copy your own affiliate link from that page and share it.
3. When someone orders through your link and pays, you earn **10% of their first payment** for that order. Renewals of that order do not earn again.

## When you can use it

- A new commission is **on hold for 14 days** after the payment. After that it shows as **Available**.
- Your very first commission, and any single commission over ₱500, is also checked by staff before it becomes available. It shows as **Being checked** until then. If staff do not approve it, it shows as **Reversed** with the reason.
- If the order is refunded or the payment is disputed during the hold, that commission is cancelled. If it is refunded after you already used the commission, the amount is taken from your next commissions.
- Available commission that you do not use **expires 12 months** after it became available. The Affiliate page shows how much expires in the next 30 days.

## Using your commission

- **Add to my credit**: any amount, instantly. Credit pays your own renewals and new orders. See [Payment methods and account credit](/billing/payment-methods).
- **Cash out to GCash or Maya** (when cash payouts are open; the Affiliate page only shows this option then): from **₱500**, up to **₱5,000 per month** (requests still waiting count toward the month). Enter your number and the name on the account; staff send it within a few days. You can cancel a request until staff start sending it.
- The Affiliate page lists every commission and payout with its status, and **CSV** downloads the full list.

- Creator partners have their own codes and discounts; see [Discounts, coupons and promo codes](/billing/discounts-and-coupons).
- Questions about the program or a commission that does not show? Open a ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com).
