---
title: "Upgrading or downgrading a server"
---

Your service page on https://billing.atbphosting.com (Services > your server) has two buttons:

- **Upgrade** shows only bigger plans. On the next step, Splits and Extra Backups can stay the same or go up.
- **Downgrade** shows only smaller plans. On the next step, Splits and Extra Backups can stay the same or go down.

A button only appears when there is somewhere to go: a server on the biggest size of its line sees no bigger plans (it can still add Splits or Extra Backups through Upgrade), and a server on the smallest size with nothing extra has no Downgrade button.

To upgrade:

1. Go to Services > your server > **Upgrade**.
2. Pick the bigger plan, then choose your Splits and Extra Backups.
3. Pay the invoice with your preferred [payment method](/billing/payment-methods). The server upgrades once the invoice is marked paid.
4. Restart the server in the panel so the new resources apply.

To downgrade:

1. Make sure your files fit the smaller storage first.
2. Go to Services > your server > **Downgrade**, pick the smaller plan and confirm.
3. The change applies right away and the unused difference goes to your account credit. Restart the server in the panel.

- **Upgrades** are charged pro rata for the days left in your current cycle. Your due date stays the same; the next renewal is at the new plan's price.
- **Downgrades** return the difference as account credit (at the discounted rate you paid), used on your next invoice.
- You can only move between sizes in the same line (Budget+ to Budget+, Premium+ to Premium+). To move from Budget+ to Premium+, open a Sales Inquiry ticket; staff move your files to the Premium+ machine. The server IP changes when it moves.
- Some sizes (especially 48 and 64 GB) can be out of stock; the Upgrade page only shows sizes that are available.
- While an upgrade invoice is unpaid you cannot start another change. Pay or cancel that invoice first.
