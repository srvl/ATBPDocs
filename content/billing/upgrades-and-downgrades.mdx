---
title: "Upgrading or downgrading a server"
description: "Move your ATBP server to a bigger or smaller plan, or between Budget+ and Premium+, from the billing area. Your files move with it."
---

Your service page on https://billing.atbphosting.com (Services > your server) groups its buttons in rows: **Server** (Manage Server), **Plan** (Upgrade and Downgrade), **Billing** (Renew, and Refund when you can still ask for one) and a red **Cancel service** button at the bottom.

![A billing service page with the Server, Plan and Billing rows: Manage Server, Upgrade, Downgrade, Renew and Cancel service](/screenshots/billing-service.webp)

The **Plan** row has two buttons:

- **Upgrade** shows bigger plans in your line, plus plans on the other line (Budget+ or Premium+) that cost more and have at least your RAM. On the next step, Splits and Extra Backups can stay the same or go up.
- **Downgrade** shows smaller plans in your line, plus plans on the other line that cost less. A cheaper Budget+ plan can have more RAM than your Premium+ plan; it still shows here, because it costs less. On the next step, Splits and Extra Backups can stay the same or go down.

A plan of the same size that costs less (for example an old Starter Bot to Bot 1 GB) shows under **Downgrade**, and one that costs more under **Upgrade**.

A button only appears when there is somewhere to go, and only while the service is paid up: if your server has an unpaid invoice, pay it first and then upgrade.

How the page is laid out:

- **Your plan** is at the top with what you pay today. Pick it to keep your plan and only change Splits or Extra Backups.
- Plans are grouped by line, one row each. Your own line comes first, then any other line (for example **Move to Premium+**), cheapest first. Each group shows its lowest price per GB of RAM, so you can compare Budget+ and Premium+ at a glance, and a group on another line says once that your server moves there.
- Each row shows the plan's price and the difference from what you pay for your plan now, for example **+₱130.00 per month** or **−₱150.00 per month**. Splits and Extra Backups are not in that difference, because they stay as they are.
- The summary on the right shows what you pay today (or get back as credit) for the days left.

To upgrade:

1. Go to Services > your server > **Upgrade**.
2. Pick the plan and press **Next Step**.

   ![The Upgrade page: Your plan at the top, then a Move to Budget+ group of plan rows with each price and its difference per month, one plan picked, and the upgrade summary with Total Today on the right](/screenshots/billing-upgrade.webp)

3. Choose your Splits and Extra Backups. The summary shows what you pay today; press **Upgrade**.

   ![The second Upgrade step with the Extra Backups and Splits choices and a summary of New setup, Unused time on current plan and Total Today](/screenshots/billing-upgrade-summary.webp)

4. Pay the invoice with your preferred [payment method](/billing/payment-methods). The server upgrades once the invoice is marked paid.
5. Restart the server in the panel so the new resources apply.

To downgrade:

1. Make sure your files fit the smaller storage first.
2. Go to Services > your server > **Downgrade**, pick the plan, press **Next Step** and confirm.

   ![The Downgrade page: Your plan at the top, a smaller Premium+ plan picked showing −₱150.00 per month, and the downgrade summary showing the credit back](/screenshots/billing-downgrade.webp)

3. The change applies right away and the unused difference goes to your account credit. Restart the server in the panel.

How the credit is worked out:

- You get back what you actually paid for the days left. If your first month was bought with a coupon (for example 25% off), those days are credited at the price you paid, not the full price.
- Free days added to your service (for example after an outage) are not credited.
- If a plan change is waiting for payment and you pay your renewal first, the plan change is cancelled, because its price was for the old end date. Open Upgrade again to get a price for the new period.

## Moving between Budget+ and Premium+

Every game (Minecraft, Hytale, Palworld, FiveM, Terraria and Valheim) can move between Budget+ and Premium+ in both directions. Budget+ and Premium+ run on different machines. When you pick a plan on the other line, your server moves there by itself: after the invoice is paid for an upgrade, or straight away for a downgrade.

A server with Splits stays on its line for now, because its split servers cannot move with it yet. The Upgrade and Downgrade pages then only show plans on your own line and say why; open a ticket if you want to change line.

- Your files, worlds and settings come with it.
- It gets a new address and port. The new ones show in the panel once the move is done. A subdomain you made in the panel moves with it, so players can keep using that.
- Extra ports get new numbers too. If a plugin or mod uses one (for example a voice chat), set its new port in that plugin's config.
- Bedrock crossplay (GeyserMC) is set up for you: Geyser gets its own Bedrock port, which is a new one on Premium+, and its config is updated to use it. Bedrock players connect with your address and that port, which shows in the panel. It applies from the next start.
- It is offline for a few minutes while the files are copied, longer for very large servers.
- The new plan's limits apply as soon as it lands. Start it again from the panel if it does not come back on its own.

Old Budget Micro servers can move up to Budget+ or Premium+ (6 GB and bigger) from the Upgrade button, and they move to the new machines the same way. Budget Micro is retired and cannot be renewed, so this is the way to keep a Budget Micro server past the end of its term; the upgraded server renews as normal. No plan can move back to old Budget.

## Upgrading to switch games

Every game costs the same per GB, but each has a smallest plan. When your plan is too small for a game, the panel's **Settings > Change Game** shows what it needs and an **Upgrade** button. That button opens the Upgrade page with only the plans big enough for that game. Pay the difference like any other upgrade, then pick the game in Change Game.

A plan change also keeps your current game in mind: a smaller plan that is below your game's smallest size (for example a 4 GB plan for a Hytale server) is refused. Switch games first, or pick a bigger plan.

## Older Premium+ plans where you picked the RAM and CPU

Premium+ servers bought before the current plans have their RAM and CPU set by you at checkout. Those amounts can no longer be changed on that plan, but everything else still works:

- **Splits and server software** can still be changed on the same plan. Your RAM and CPU stay exactly as they are, and you only pay for what you changed.
- **To change size, move to one of the current [Premium+ plans](/plans/premium-plus-plans-and-prices).** They show on the Upgrade and Downgrade pages. Which page a plan is on depends on its price compared with what you pay today, not only on its RAM: a current plan that costs more is an Upgrade, one that costs less is a Downgrade.
- The move stays on the same machines, so your address, port and files do not change. The new plan's RAM, CPU, storage and limits apply as soon as it is done.
- Moving to a current plan is one way: you cannot go back to the older plan afterwards.
- The price is worked out the same way as any other change (below): what you pay today, including your RAM and CPU, is credited for the days left.


## How the price is worked out

The summary on the Upgrade or Downgrade page shows two lines before the total:

- **New setup**: what the new plan and options cost per day, times the days left until your due date.
- **Unused time on current plan**: what you are billed per day today, times the same days. This counts your term discount, any coupon and any paid Splits or Extra Backups, including ones the new plan does not have.

The total is the first line minus the second. Days are counted on the real length of your billing cycle (31 days for a 31-day month), so a day costs the same whichever month you are in.

Example: a Budget+ 6 GB server at ₱210 a month, due on 31 October, moves to Premium+ 6 GB (₱450) on 10 October. 21 of the cycle's 31 days are left. New setup is ₱450 / 31 x 21 = ₱304.84, unused time is ₱210 / 31 x 21 = ₱142.26, so the upgrade invoice is ₱162.58. Moving straight back the same day returns ₱162.58 as account credit.

- Your due date stays the same; the next renewal is at the new plan's price.
- **Downgrades** return the difference as account credit, used on your next invoice.
- If you renewed several months ahead at a discount, the days you prepaid keep that discount on both sides: unused time is credited at the rate you paid, and the new plan runs on those days at the same discount.
- A change made on your due date costs nothing today; the new price starts with the next invoice.
- An unpaid upgrade invoice is re-priced every day, so it always matches the days still left when you pay.
- An upgrade on the same tier grows your server on the machine it is already on, so the Upgrade page offers a bigger plan only when that machine has room for it. Stock on the order page is for new servers on any machine, so a plan can show in stock there and still be missing from your Upgrade page while your machine is full. It also works the other way: some servers can move up to a size that is sold out for new orders, depending on what the server really uses and how much room its machine has (for example, a bigger plan with the same vCPU). What the Upgrade page offers can change from day to day. A smaller plan on the same machines is always offered on the Downgrade page.
- While an upgrade invoice is unpaid you cannot start another change, and the Upgrade page will not open. Pay that invoice first. If you picked the wrong plan and the invoice has no cancel option, open a ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) and staff will remove it so you can choose again.
