Pay at https://billing.atbphosting.com under Invoices, or add credit first under **Account > Credits**. We accept:

![The Credits page: Top up your balance with 1. How will you pay? and a card each for GCash, Maya, Crypto and Bank transfer, tagged Automatic or Manual, next to Your balance](/screenshots/credits-open-page.webp)

- **GCash**. Send from the number saved on your account and it is added automatically, or get an exact amount on the Credits page and send it from any GCash account. See [GCash](/billing/gcash).

  ![Credits page, 2. Send the money with GCash picked: the GCash number with a Copy button, and the Show QR code button](/screenshots/credits-gcash-number.webp)

- **Maya / PayMaya**. When you pay an invoice with Maya you get an exact amount to send, usually just the amount due, with a 15-minute timer. Send exactly that from any verified Maya account and it is added automatically, even if Maya shows your username (for example @juan) instead of your number. A payment from the number saved on your account is matched too. On the Credits page you can send from your saved number, or get an exact amount there (use that if Maya shows your username). Use Maya's normal **Send money**, not a **Money Card**: Maya does not show us the amount of a Money Card, so staff add it by hand after checking, which is not instant.
  - **Unverified / Maya Basic accounts:** Maya Basic (unverified) accounts cannot use Maya's Send Money feature, so they cannot pay to our Maya number or Maya QR code (our Maya QR is a personal Send Money QR, not a merchant QRPH code). Upgrade/verify your Maya account first, or use GCash, bank transfer, or a card through **Stripe** instead.
  - **Maya Virtual Card or Maya debit card:** Choose **Card (Stripe)** at checkout to pay with a Maya Virtual Card or Maya debit/credit card — do not choose the Maya wallet Send Money option.

  ![Credits page, 2. Send the money with Maya picked: the Maya number, the You send from box for the number you send from, and Sending from another number or a bank?](/screenshots/credits-maya.webp)

- **Bank transfer** from any Philippine bank over InstaPay, to our Maya or our GCash, credited automatically. To Maya you send an exact amount we give you; to GCash you enter the last 4 digits of your bank account number (not your card). See [Bank transfer](/billing/bank).

  ![Credits page, 2. Send the money with Bank transfer picked, To Maya: enter the pesos to add or tap an amount, then press Get exact amount](/screenshots/credits-bank-transfer-to-maya.webp)

- **PayPal** and **card (Stripe)** at checkout. You can save either one so renewals are charged automatically. To turn that on for a server, open billing > Services > your server, find **Auto paying using** (it says **Not configured** until you set it), press the pencil next to it and pick your saved card or PayPal. When you pay an invoice, your saved cards and PayPal show as cards (with a **Cards** / **PayPal** tab when you have both). The one that already renews that server is marked **Default** and picked for you; tap another one to pay with it instead. You can also turn on **Renew automatically with this card** when you pay an invoice with a saved card or PayPal; switch it off any time on the server's page.
  - **Card refused or "3D Secure failed"?** 3D Secure is your bank checking that the card owner is paying; when it fails, no money is taken. Open the invoice again, press **Pay**, and finish your bank's OTP or app prompt in the window that opens (allow pop-ups for the billing site). The card must be in your own name with online payments turned on, and the bank may send the code to the phone number it has on file. If your bank still declines it, pay with GCash or Maya instead. Staff cannot pass the bank's check for you.
- **Crypto**, when paying an invoice or on the Credits page. Choose **Crypto**, then pick the coin from the list; right now that is **Litecoin (LTC)**. You get an exact amount of that coin to send from any exchange or wallet; it is credited after the network confirms it. See [Paying with crypto](/billing/crypto).

  ![Credits page, 2. Send the money with Crypto picked: Litecoin (LTC) on Litecoin, enter the pesos to add and press Get exact amount](/screenshots/credits-crypto.webp)

## Auto and Manual

When you pay an invoice, PayPal, Stripe, GCash, Maya, Bank transfer and Crypto are listed separately, each with a badge:

- **Auto** (green): your credit is added automatically, usually within a minute.
- **Manual** (yellow): staff confirm it. For GCash, upload your receipt on the payment screen or the Credits page after paying (see [When GCash shows Manual](/billing/gcash#when-gcash-shows-manual)). For other methods, open a **General Inquiry** ticket on [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with your receipt and account email.

  ![Credits page when GCash is Manual: the Upload your GCash receipt box with the amount you sent and the Choose picture button](/screenshots/credits-gcash-upload-receipt.webp)

On the Credits page, step **1. How will you pay?** shows a card for each method, tagged **Automatic** (added by itself) or **Manual** (staff add it), with one line on how it works. Pick a card and step **2. Send the money** shows what to send for that method. Inside Bank transfer, **To Maya** or **To GCash** carries a **Manual** tag whenever that wallet is Manual. A method can switch to Manual for a while, for example if GCash notifications are delayed. GCash and Maya do this by themselves: if an automatic top-up is not matched within a few minutes, or the payment service stops answering, the method switches to Manual, and it switches back to Auto once automatic top-ups work again. The tag always shows the current state.

## Receipts

- With an **Auto** method you do not need to send anything. The credit or the paid invoice shows in billing, usually within a minute.
- Keep your receipt (a screenshot with the reference number, amount and time) until you see the credit. If nothing shows after a few minutes, or the method was **Manual**, upload the receipt (GCash) or open a **General Inquiry** ticket on [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with the receipt, your invoice number and your account email.
- Staff add a payment by its **reference number**, and each receipt can be credited once. Send the original receipt with the reference number visible; a receipt that was already credited, to any account, is not credited again.
- If staff added a payment by hand and the same amount then also arrives automatically within a day, the second one is checked before it is added, so a payment is never counted twice. A real second payment of the same amount is still added once staff confirm it.

## Exact amounts

For an exact-amount top-up you choose how many pesos to add and get an amount with extra centavos (for example **₱500.37**) and a 15-minute timer. A Maya invoice payment and a bank transfer to Maya work the same way, except that you usually send just the amount due or the amount you chose; centavos are added only if someone else is paying the same amount at that moment. Send exactly that amount before the timer ends and it is matched to your account, whoever sends it. A different amount cannot be matched automatically; open a **General Inquiry** ticket with your receipt.

## Account credit

- Prices are in Philippine pesos. Billing also shows US dollars, converted nightly; the peso price is what is charged. 1 credit = 1 PHP.
- **Account credit** is paid in advance. Credit that covers the whole amount when a renewal invoice is created pays it automatically. Credit added after that stays on your balance until you pay the invoice with it. Credit cannot be moved to another account and is not normally refunded as cash; the [refund policy](/billing/refund-policy) covers the exceptions, such as unspent credit we can trace to a real cash payment.
- **Your balance** is the first thing on the Credits page (at the top on a phone, on the right on a computer). It updates by itself, so a top-up shows up while the page is open.
- **Balance history**: the Credits page lists every change to your credit, newest first: top-ups, invoices paid with credit (with the invoice number), refunds and promo codes, with your balance after each one. It shows the latest 8; press **Show all** to see the rest.
- There are no installment plans: an invoice is paid in full. You can add credit bit by bit before it is due, then pay the invoice from your credit once it covers the whole amount.
- Payment not showing? Link your Discord with `/billing link`, then open a **General Inquiry** ticket with your invoice number and receipt. Once staff add the credit, the bot usually posts your balance and invoice link in the ticket; open the invoice and use **Account Credits** under **Pay with Credits**. See [Paying with GCash](/billing/gcash#paid-but-the-invoice-still-says-waiting-for-payment).
- **How do I know it is really ATBP?** Check the number, not the name. If the GCash or Maya number matches the one on your invoice or the Credits page at https://billing.atbphosting.com, it is ours, even when the account name in your app does not say ATBP Hosting. Scanning the QR code there is safest, because the number cannot be mistyped. A number sent to you in a DM that does not match is not ours.
- Venmo, Cash App and sending money directly to staff are not accepted. International customers can use PayPal, a card through Stripe, or crypto (Litecoin). You can pay from any currency, including Indian rupees, as long as you use one of the methods listed on this page.

<Callout type="info">

**Paid but the invoice still says unpaid?** Top-ups land on your account credit. Open the invoice, click **Account Credits** under **Pay with Credits**, then **Apply Credits and Pay**. If your credit covers only part of it, add the rest. An open invoice page updates by itself, so you do not need to refresh it. See [GCash](/billing/gcash).
