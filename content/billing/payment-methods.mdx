---
title: "Payment methods and account credit"
---

Pay at https://billing.atbphosting.com under Invoices, or add credit first under **Account > Credits**. We accept:

- **GCash**. Send from the number saved on your account and it is added automatically, or get an exact amount on the Credits page and send it from any GCash account. See [GCash](/billing/gcash).
- **Maya / PayMaya**. Get an exact amount on the Credits page and send it from any Maya account; it is added automatically.
- **USDT on TRON (TRC-20)**. Get an exact USDT amount on the Credits page and send it from any exchange or wallet; it is added after the network confirms it. See [USDT (crypto)](/billing/crypto).
- **Any Philippine bank** that can send money to GCash or Maya. See [Bank transfer](/billing/bank).
- **PayPal** and **card (Stripe)** at checkout. You can save either one so renewals are charged automatically.

## Auto and Manual

Every method on the Credits page and at checkout has a badge:

- **Auto** (green): your credit is added automatically, usually within a minute (USDT within a few minutes).
- **Manual** (yellow): staff confirm it. After paying, open a Billing ticket on [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with your receipt and account email.

A method can switch to Manual for a while, for example if GCash notifications are delayed. The badge always shows the current state.

## Exact amounts

For an exact-amount top-up you choose how many pesos to add, and the Credits page gives you an amount with extra centavos (for example **₱500.37**) or a USDT amount (for example **8.7028 USDT**) and a 15-minute timer. Send exactly that amount before the timer ends and it is matched to your account, whoever sends it. A different amount cannot be matched automatically; open a Billing ticket with your receipt.

## Account credit

- Prices are in Philippine pesos. Billing also shows US dollars, converted nightly; the peso price is what is charged. 1 credit = 1 PHP.
- **Account credit** is paid in advance and is used automatically for your next invoice. Credit has no cash value and cannot be moved to another account.
- Payment not showing? Open a Billing ticket with your invoice number and receipt.
