---
title: "Payment methods and account credit"
---

Pay at https://billing.atbphosting.com under Invoices. We accept:

- **GCash** (automatic top-up; it is having issues right now, so if your credit doesn't arrive, open a Billing ticket with your receipt).
- **Maya / PayMaya** (automatic top-up).
- **Any Philippine bank** that can send money to GCash or Maya.
- **PayPal** and **card (Stripe)** at checkout. You can save either one so renewals are charged automatically.
- **Crypto** through a Billing ticket (automatic crypto payments are coming soon).

- Prices are in Philippine pesos. Billing also shows US dollars, converted nightly; the peso price is what is charged.
- **Account credit** is paid in advance and is used automatically for your next invoice. Credit has no cash value and cannot be moved to another account.
- Payment not showing? Open a Billing ticket with your invoice number and receipt.
