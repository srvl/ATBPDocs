Pay at https://billing.atbphosting.com under Invoices, or add credit first under **Account > Credits**. We accept:

- **GCash**. Send from the number saved on your account and it is added automatically, or get an exact amount on the Credits page and send it from any GCash account. See [GCash](/billing/gcash).
- **Maya / PayMaya**. When you pay an invoice with Maya you get an exact amount to send, usually just the amount due, with a 15-minute timer. Send exactly that from any Maya account and it is added automatically, even if Maya shows your username (for example @juan) instead of your number. A payment from the number saved on your account is matched too. On the Credits page you can send from your saved number, or get an exact amount there (use that if Maya shows your username). Use Maya's normal **Send money**, not a **Money Card**: Maya does not show us the amount of a Money Card, so staff add it by hand after checking, which is not instant.
- **Bank transfer** from any Philippine bank over InstaPay, to our Maya or our GCash, credited automatically. To Maya you send an exact amount we give you; to GCash you enter the last 4 digits of your bank account number (not your card). See [Bank transfer](/billing/bank).
- **PayPal** and **card (Stripe)** at checkout. You can save either one so renewals are charged automatically.
- **Litecoin (LTC)**, when paying an invoice or on the Credits page. You get an exact LTC amount to send from any exchange or wallet; it is credited after the network confirms it. See [Litecoin (crypto)](/billing/crypto).

## Auto and Manual

When you pay an invoice, PayPal, Stripe, GCash, Maya, Bank transfer and Litecoin are listed separately, each with a badge:

- **Auto** (green): your credit is added automatically, usually within a minute.
- **Manual** (yellow): staff confirm it. After paying, open a **General Inquiry** ticket on [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with your receipt and account email.

On the Credits page each method has its own tab, and only a method that staff confirm right now carries a yellow **Manual** tag. Bank transfer shows the tag per destination: a bank transfer to Maya or GCash is Manual whenever that wallet is. A method can switch to Manual for a while, for example if GCash notifications are delayed. GCash and Maya do this by themselves: if an automatic top-up is not matched within a few minutes, or the payment service stops answering, the method switches to Manual, and it switches back to Auto once automatic top-ups work again. The tag always shows the current state.

## Receipts

- With an **Auto** method you do not need to send anything. The credit or the paid invoice shows in billing, usually within a minute.
- Keep your receipt (a screenshot with the reference number, amount and time) until you see the credit. If nothing shows after a few minutes, or the method was **Manual**, open a **General Inquiry** ticket on [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with the receipt, your invoice number and your account email.
- Staff add a payment by its **reference number**, and each receipt can be credited once. Send the original receipt with the reference number visible; a receipt that was already credited, to any account, is not credited again.
- If staff added a payment by hand and the same amount then also arrives automatically within a day, the second one is checked before it is added, so a payment is never counted twice. A real second payment of the same amount is still added once staff confirm it.

## Exact amounts

For an exact-amount top-up you choose how many pesos to add and get an amount with extra centavos (for example **₱500.37**) and a 15-minute timer. A Maya invoice payment and a bank transfer to Maya work the same way, except that you usually send just the amount due or the amount you chose; centavos are added only if someone else is paying the same amount at that moment. Send exactly that amount before the timer ends and it is matched to your account, whoever sends it. A different amount cannot be matched automatically; open a **General Inquiry** ticket with your receipt.

## Account credit

- Prices are in Philippine pesos. Billing also shows US dollars, converted nightly; the peso price is what is charged. 1 credit = 1 PHP.
- **Account credit** is paid in advance and is used automatically for your next invoice. Credit has no cash value and cannot be moved to another account.
- Payment not showing? Link your Discord with `/billing link`, then open a **General Inquiry** ticket with your invoice number and receipt. Once staff add the credit, the bot usually posts your balance and invoice link in the ticket; open the invoice and use **Account Credits** under **Pay with Credits**. See [Paying with GCash](/billing/gcash#paid-but-the-invoice-still-says-waiting-for-payment).
- Venmo, Cash App and sending money directly to staff are not accepted. International customers can use PayPal, a card through Stripe, or LTC.

<Callout type="info">

**Paid but the invoice still says unpaid?** Credit pays an open invoice for your server by itself once it covers the whole amount, usually within seconds and at most 10 minutes after it arrives. If your credit covers only part of it, add the rest, or open the invoice, click **Account Credits**, then **Apply Credits and Pay**. See [GCash](/billing/gcash).
