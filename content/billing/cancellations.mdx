## How to cancel

1. Download a backup of all your files from the panel.
2. On https://billing.atbphosting.com open **Services**, pick the server and press the red **Cancel service** button at the bottom.
3. Pick the **Cancellation Type** and give a reason:
   - **Cancel at the end of the billing period**: the server keeps running until the last day you already paid for, then it is removed. No renewal invoice is charged.
   - **Cancel immediate**: the server is cancelled and deleted straight away. It cannot be renewed or brought back.
4. Press **Cancel** to confirm.

![The cancellation window with the Cancellation Type choice, the reason box and the Cancel button](/screenshots/billing-cancel.webp)

Changed your mind about an end-of-period cancellation? Open a **Billing** ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) before the last day.

You can also simply not pay the next invoice. The invoice is created 7 days before the due date, the server is suspended on the due date if it is unpaid, and it is deleted 3 days after the due date. See [renewals, suspension and deletion](/billing/renewals-suspension-and-deletion).

<Callout type="warning">

Once a cancellation is processed or a server is deleted, the server, its databases and its panel backups are gone. We keep one saved backup of its files for 30 days: billing > Services > the server > **Download your files** (files only, no databases). Within those 30 days staff can also restore the server from that backup if you open a ticket. After that nothing can be recovered.

</Callout>

## Refunds

Cancelling does not automatically refund you. To ask for money back, use the **Refund** button instead of **Cancel service**. If you are within 3 days of purchase, or think you qualify, read [how to request a refund](/billing/refunds) and the [refund policy](/billing/refund-policy).
