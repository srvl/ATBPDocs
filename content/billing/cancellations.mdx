## How to cancel

1. Download a backup of all your files from the panel.
2. Open a **Billing** ticket in our [Discord server](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with your billing account email and the server you want to cancel.
3. Say whether you want to cancel now or let it run until the end of the period you already paid for.

You can also simply not pay the next invoice. The invoice is created 7 days before the due date, the server is suspended on the due date if it is unpaid, and it is deleted after the grace period. See [renewals, suspension and deletion](/billing/renewals-suspension-and-deletion).

<Callout type="warning">

Once a cancellation is processed or a server is deleted, all server files are gone and cannot be recovered.

</Callout>

## Refunds

Cancelling does not automatically refund you. If you are within 3 days of purchase, or think you qualify, read [how to request a refund](/billing/refunds) and the [refund policy](/billing/refund-policy).
