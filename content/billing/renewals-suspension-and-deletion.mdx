---
title: "Renewals, overdue invoices, suspension and deletion"
description: "When renewal invoices are created, when an unpaid server is suspended and deleted, and how to get your saved files back."
---

import { Callout } from 'nextra/components'

- A renewal invoice is created 7 days before your due date. If you link your Discord with `/billing link`, the bot also reminds you.
- If your account credit covers the invoice when it is created, or you have a saved PayPal/Stripe payment method, the invoice is paid automatically. Credit you add after that does not pay it by itself: open the invoice and pay it from your credit.
- **Renew early or in advance:** open billing > Services > your server and press **Renew**. Pick how long to renew for (one billing period, or several months at once with the same term discounts as new orders), enter a coupon code if you have one, and press **Create renewal invoice**. Your due date moves forward as soon as that invoice is paid.

![A billing service page with the Renew button under Billing circled](/screenshots/billing-service-renew.webp)

![The Renew page: Renew now and Prepay 3, 6 and 12 month rows with their prices, Prepay 3 months marked, and the Renewal summary with the Coupon box and the Create renewal invoice button marked](/screenshots/billing-renew.webp)

- You can also just pay the renewal invoice before the due date. Once a renewal invoice exists, the **Renew** page works on that invoice instead of making a new one: it is where you add a coupon code to it (the invoice also has a **Have a coupon code?** link under **Pay**). See [coupons](/billing/discounts-and-coupons).
- **If the invoice is still unpaid on the due date, the server is suspended** (it stops, and every tab of the server in the panel shows a suspended page with a button to pay). Billing runs on Canadian Mountain time, so suspensions happen around 2 PM to 3 PM Philippine time on the due date.
- **When a server is suspended, we save a backup of its files.** The server is stopped first, so worlds are saved in a clean state.
- **3 days after the due date, an unpaid server is deleted**, together with its databases and the backups you made in the panel. The suspension email says how long you have, and a reminder is emailed about a day before.
- **Need more time to pay? Extensions are case by case.** Open a ticket before the deadline. If we have enough stock we may be able to give you more time, but when stock is short and demand is high the answer will most likely be no. If you cannot pay in time, you can still download your files and ask for the server back as explained below.
- Paying the overdue invoice before deletion unsuspends the server automatically, with everything on it. This is always the best option if you want to keep playing.
- **Need your files but cannot renew?** Open billing > Services > your server. The **Saved backup of your files** box has a **Download your files** button. It works while the server is suspended and for **30 days after it is deleted**. The suspension and deletion emails have the same button. The download is a `.tar` file of the server's files (worlds, configs, plugins); databases are not included. After those 30 days nothing is left to download.

  ![Billing service page of a deleted server: Saved backup of your files, the date it is available until and the Download your files button](/screenshots/billing-saved-backup-download.webp)

- **Want the deleted server itself back?** Within the same 30 days, open a ticket in our [Discord server](https://discord.atbphosting.com) and staff can restore the server from its saved backup (files only, no databases).
- **10 days after the due date, we email you once** to say the files of a deleted server are still saved and how to get them back. It only goes out if a saved backup exists and you have no other server with us. You can turn it off in your notification settings.
- Your billing account is not deleted when a server expires; only that server is.
- **Retired plans cannot be renewed.** Right now that is the old Budget Nano and Budget Micro. Their service page shows a notice instead of **Renew**, no renewal invoice is created, and the status reads **Cancellation pending** even though you did not cancel. The server runs until its due date, then it is suspended and deleted as above. To keep it, **Upgrade** it to a current plan before then; see [Budget+ plans](/plans/budget-plus-plans-and-prices).

<Callout type="info">

**Paid but the invoice still says unpaid?** Top-ups land on your account credit. Open the invoice, click **Account Credits** under **Pay with Credits**, then **Apply Credits and Pay**. If your credit covers only part of it, add the rest. An open invoice page updates by itself, so you do not need to refresh it. See [GCash](/billing/gcash).

</Callout>
