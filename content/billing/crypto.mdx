---
title: "Paying with USDT (crypto)"
---

You can add credit with **USDT on the TRON network (TRC-20)**, sent from any exchange (Binance, coins.ph, Bybit and others) or your own wallet. There are no gateway fees on our side.

## Steps

1. Open the [Credits page](https://billing.atbphosting.com/account/credits) and choose **USDT (TRC-20)**.
2. Enter how many pesos to add and press **Get my exact amount**.
3. You get our TRON address, an exact USDT amount (for example **8.7028 USDT**) and a 15-minute timer. The peso amount you get is fixed when you press the button.
4. Send **exactly** that USDT amount, on the **TRON (TRC-20)** network, before the timer ends.
5. Your credit is added after the network confirms the transfer, usually within a few minutes. The page updates on its own.

## Before you send

- **USDT on TRC-20 only.** Other coins, or USDT on another network (ERC-20, BEP-20, Solana), cannot be credited and may not be recoverable.
- **Exchange fees.** Most exchanges take the withdrawal fee out of the amount you enter. Add the fee on top so the exact amount arrives.
- **Wrong amount or late?** It cannot be matched automatically. Open a Billing ticket on [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with the transaction ID (TXID).

If USDT shows the yellow **Manual** badge, send to the address shown and then open a Billing ticket with the TXID and your account email.
