---
title: "Paying with Litecoin (crypto)"
---

You can pay invoices and add credit with **Litecoin (LTC)**, sent from any exchange (Binance, coins.ph, Bybit and others) or your own wallet. There are no gateway fees on our side.

## Paying an invoice

When you pay an invoice, choose **Litecoin**. You get the exact LTC amount for what the invoice owes, our address and a 15-minute timer. Once the network confirms the transfer (usually within 10 minutes), the invoice is marked paid.

## Adding credit

1. Open the [Credits page](https://billing.atbphosting.com/account/credits) and choose **Litecoin (LTC)**.
2. Enter how many pesos to add and press **Get exact amount**.
3. You get our LTC address, an exact LTC amount (for example **0.12495100 LTC**) and a 15-minute timer. The peso amount you get is fixed when you press the button.
4. Send **exactly** that LTC amount, on the **Litecoin** network, before the timer ends.
5. Your credit is added after the network confirms the transfer (3 confirmations), usually within 10 minutes. The page updates on its own.

## Before you send

- **LTC on the Litecoin network only.** Other coins, or LTC on another network, cannot be credited and may not be recoverable.
- **Exchange fees.** Most exchanges take the withdrawal fee out of the amount you enter. Add the fee on top so the exact amount arrives.
- **The price.** The LTC amount is based on the current market price plus a small margin, fixed for the 15 minutes.
- **Wrong amount or late?** It cannot be matched automatically. Open a Billing ticket on [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with the transaction ID (TXID).

If Litecoin shows the yellow **Manual** tag, send to the address shown and then open a Billing ticket with the TXID and your account email.
