## Where do I see my due date?

Log in at [billing.atbphosting.com](https://billing.atbphosting.com) and open Services or Invoices. Renewal invoices are generated 7 days before the due date.

## When exactly is it due?

Billing runs on Edmonton time at 00:00, which is around 2 PM Philippine time. It shifts by an hour with daylight saving time.

## Suspension

If the invoice is unpaid on the due date, the server is suspended. You cannot start it or use its files in the panel. No files are deleted while it is suspended. Paying the invoice unsuspends it automatically.

When the server is suspended it is stopped and we save a backup of its files. Paying the invoice removes that saved backup again, because your server is back with everything on it.

## Termination

An unpaid server is deleted 3 days after its due date. Because suspension happens on the due date, that is also 3 days after it was suspended. You get an email when the server is suspended and a reminder about a day before it is deleted. Deleting removes the server, its databases and the backups you made in the panel.

## Your saved backup

The backup saved at suspension stays downloadable for **30 days after the server is deleted**:

1. Log in at [billing.atbphosting.com](https://billing.atbphosting.com) and open **Services**, then the server.
2. In the **Saved backup of your files** box, press **Download your files**. The suspension and deletion emails have the same button.
3. You get a `.tar` file of the server's files (worlds, configs, plugins). Open it with 7-Zip, WinRAR or any archive tool. Databases are not included.

If the box says the backup is being saved or prepared, check back in a few minutes. If it says the backup could not be saved, open a ticket in our [Discord server](https://discord.atbphosting.com) before the deletion date and staff will help. A server with splits gets a saved backup for each split too.

The same saved backup is kept when you cancel a server or it ends at the end of its term.
