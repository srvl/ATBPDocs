Active Minecraft and gaming creators can partner with ATBP Hosting.

## Perks

- **A free or discounted hosting plan:** a baseline server at no cost, scaled to your audience size and engagement.
- **Your own creator code:** a coupon code that gives your community a discount.
- **Affiliate rewards:** credits when someone signs up and buys a plan through your [affiliate link](/billing/affiliate-program), on their first month.

## Requirements

- At least **1,000 followers** on any platform (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Twitch and so on). Below that, open a ticket anyway and we can talk about it.
- You actively make content for Minecraft or another game we host.
- You post at least **one piece of content a week** showcasing ATBP Hosting.
- Good engagement and consistent activity.

## How to apply

Open a **Creators Partnership Program** ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) and tell us about your channels. Staff review them in the ticket. Creator codes are private, so they are not listed on [Current promotions](/billing/current-promotions).
