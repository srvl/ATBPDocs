Crossplay only works on dedicated servers, with a maximum of 8 players, and **no mods**.

## Settings

Stop the server, edit `serverconfig.xml` in **Files**, and set:

- `ServerAllowCrossplay` to `true`
- `EACEnabled` to `true`
- `IgnoreEOSSanctions` to `false`
- `ServerMaxPlayerCount` to 8 or lower
- `ServerVisibility` to `2`
- `WorldGenSize` to 8192 or lower

Save and start the server.

## Extra ports

Console players use your game port plus the next three. With game port 26900 that is 26901, 26902 and 26903. Ask for them in a ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) and say you are setting up crossplay.

<Callout type="info">

If the server does not start, check the log for "Server configuration is invalid". It means one of the requirements above is missing. Test with a console player before inviting everyone.

</Callout>
