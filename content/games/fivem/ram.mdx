title: "How much RAM does a FiveM server need?"
description: "Rough RAM guidance for FiveM by server type and player count."
---

These are rough estimates, not official numbers. Watch usage in the [panel](https://panel.atbphosting.com) and txAdmin, and upgrade when you need to.

| Server | Approximate RAM |
| --- | --- |
| Small or test server, a few friends | 4 GB (Budget+) or 6 GB (Premium+) |
| QBCore or ESX roleplay, 20 to 32 players | 6 GB |
| Busy roleplay with many scripts and custom maps, up to 48 players | 8 GB |
| Heavy servers with lots of streamed cars and maps | 12 to 16 GB |

FiveM plans come in 4 to 16 GB on Budget+ (AMD EPYC 7B13, ₱35 per GB) and 6 to 16 GB on Premium+ (Ryzen 9 9950X, ₱75 per GB). Every plan includes backups and its own port for txAdmin. Storage is 10 GB per 1 GB of RAM on Premium+, and 5 GB per 1 GB on Budget+ (never less than 50 GB). Prices are on [Budget+ plans and prices](/plans/budget-plus-plans-and-prices) and [Premium+ plans and prices](/plans/premium-plus-plans-and-prices).

## What raises usage

- Streamed cars, clothing and maps, which the server holds in memory.
- Many scripts running loops all the time.
- More players online at once.

A free Cfx.re key allows up to 48 players, so going past 16 GB rarely helps.
