1. Open your server in the [panel](https://panel.atbphosting.com) and copy the **IP address** and **port**. Bedrock normally uses port `19132`, but use the one shown for your server.
2. Start **Minecraft Bedrock Edition** (mobile, Windows, or console) and press **Play**.
3. Open the **Servers** tab, scroll the list to the bottom, and choose **Add Server**.
4. Fill in a name, the address (without the port), and the port. Save, or press **Play** to join straight away.

<Callout type="info">

Bedrock servers only accept Bedrock players. Java Edition players cannot join unless you add Geyser, see [GeyserMC](/plugins_and_modifications/plugins/geysermc).

</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

- Check the server shows as online in the panel.
- Look for typos or extra spaces in the address and port.
- Make sure the port is in its own box, not typed into the address.
- Consoles cannot add servers directly, see [joining from console](/games/minecraft-bedrock/consoles).

## Bedrock players suddenly cannot join

- Check https://status.atbphosting.com first. A network incident can stop players from joining even though your server is fine.
- **Some players can join and others cannot?** Have the ones who cannot join update Minecraft. Players on Bedrock 26.50 or older cannot join a NetherNet server. If they are up to date and still cannot join, see [Diagnosing Ping Issues](/extras/ping-issues) and send their MTR or traceroute in a ticket.
- **Only one player can join after a restart, a player cannot rejoin, or an 11th player cannot join (often with a "Door" or NetherNet error)?** See [NetherNet](#nethernet) below: every player online needs a port from your server's player port block. This usually means ports were deleted from that block.
- **"Terracotta" error (`InitialConnection-149`)?** The player's game could not finish the NetherNet connection. Have the player update Minecraft and join with your subdomain (see [NetherNet](#nethernet) below), then try mobile data. If it keeps happening, send the time and the player's public IP in a ticket so we can trace the connection.
- **"Trust this server?" on first join, or some players get stuck connecting?** Have players join with your server's subdomain instead of the IP, see [NetherNet](#nethernet) below.
- **Stuck on the loading screen?** Remove addons one by one to find the one causing it, see [Bedrock Addons](/running_a_server/bedrock-addons).
- **After changing or deleting a port?** Any subdomain still points at the old port. Delete the subdomain and create it again for the new port.
- **Java and Bedrock on the same server?** Bedrock players join with the Bedrock port, which can differ from the Java port.

## NetherNet

Bedrock 26.60 and newer only connect with Bedrock's new network transport, NetherNet. Your Bedrock server is already set up for it: the panel picks it under **Startup**, **Network transport**, and fills in the ports and addresses Bedrock needs on every start. You don't need to change anything in `server.properties`. Seeing a long list of ports there is normal: the panel writes them on every start, so leave them as they are.

- **One port per player online.** NetherNet gives every player online their own port, so your Bedrock server comes with a block of 10 player ports in a row (for example 19300 to 19309) on top of its main port, which is 10 players at the same time. Bedrock only uses ports that follow on from each other, so don't delete ports from the block, even if you don't recognise them. If ports were deleted, stop the server, add them back with **Add ports** on the block's line in the **Network** tab, and start it again. For more players, open the **Network** tab, click **Add ports** on your block's line and choose how many; they join onto your block (see [Ports in a row](/using_the_panel/adding-ports#ports-in-a-row)). Player ports don't count toward your port limit, and a server can have up to 50, which is 50 players at the same time. Need more? Open a ticket. Players always join with your main address and port.
- **Join with your subdomain.** Players who join with a subdomain from the panel (see [Custom domains and subdomains](/general/custom-domains-and-subdomains)) connect securely and are never asked to trust your server. Players who join with the IP address may be asked to trust it the first time, and some Bedrock versions don't connect by IP at all, so give players the subdomain. Your server picks this up at its next restart.
- **RakNet** (the old transport) only works for players on Bedrock 26.50 or older. You can still pick it under **Network transport**, but newer players then cannot join.
- Java servers with Geyser are set up for NetherNet too, see [GeyserMC](/plugins_and_modifications/plugins/geysermc).
