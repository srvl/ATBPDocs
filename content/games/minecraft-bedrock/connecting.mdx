1. Open your server in the [panel](https://panel.atbphosting.com) and copy the **IP address** and **port**. Bedrock normally uses port `19132`, but use the one shown for your server.
2. Start **Minecraft Bedrock Edition** (mobile, Windows, or console) and press **Play**.
3. Open the **Servers** tab, scroll the list to the bottom, and choose **Add Server**.
4. Fill in a name, the address (without the port), and the port. Save, or press **Play** to join straight away.

<Callout type="info">

Bedrock servers only accept Bedrock players. Java Edition players cannot join unless you add Geyser, see [GeyserMC](/plugins_and_modifications/plugins/geysermc).

</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

- Check the server shows as online in the panel.
- Look for typos or extra spaces in the address and port.
- Make sure the port is in its own box, not typed into the address.
- Consoles cannot add servers directly, see [joining from console](/games/minecraft-bedrock/consoles).

## Bedrock players suddenly cannot join

- Check https://status.atbphosting.com first. A network incident can stop players from joining even though your server is fine.
- **A 6th player cannot join, or players time out right after connecting?** See [NetherNet](#nethernet) below: every player online needs a port.
- **"Trust this server?" on first join?** That is normal on NetherNet, see below. Players press trust once.
- **Stuck on the loading screen?** Remove addons one by one to find the one causing it, see [Bedrock Addons](/running_a_server/bedrock-addons).
- **After changing or deleting a port?** Any subdomain still points at the old port. Delete the subdomain and create it again for the new port.
- **Java and Bedrock on the same server?** Bedrock players join with the Bedrock port, which can differ from the Java port.

## NetherNet

Bedrock 26.60 and newer only connect with Bedrock's new network transport, NetherNet. Your Bedrock server is already set up for it: the panel picks it under **Startup**, **Network transport**, and fills in the ports and addresses Bedrock needs on every start. You don't need to change anything in `server.properties`.

- **One port per player online.** NetherNet gives every player online their own port, so your Bedrock server comes with 5 ports, which is 5 players at the same time. For more, add ports on the **Network** tab, choosing **Bedrock players (NetherNet)**, or **Other games** if that choice isn't in your list (see [Adding Ports](/using_the_panel/adding-ports)), then restart. Players still join with your main address and port.
- **"Trust this server?"** The first time a player joins they may be asked to trust your server. They press trust once and are not asked again, even after restarts.
- **RakNet** (the old transport) only works for players on Bedrock 26.50 or older. You can still pick it under **Network transport**, but newer players then cannot join.
- Java servers with Geyser are set up for NetherNet too, see [GeyserMC](/plugins_and_modifications/plugins/geysermc).
