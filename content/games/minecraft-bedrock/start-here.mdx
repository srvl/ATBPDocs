Never run a server before? Follow this page from top to bottom.

**You need:** Minecraft Bedrock Edition on a phone, Windows or console. Java Edition cannot join a Bedrock server.

## 1. Log in to the game panel

Your server runs on the game panel at https://panel.atbphosting.com, not on the billing site. They are separate accounts with separate passwords.

- Your panel login is the email in the **"Your server is ready"** message. Check spam if you cannot find it.
- No panel password yet? Look for the email titled **Panel password inside**, or press **Forgot password** on the panel login page.
- From billing you can also open **Services**, pick your server and press **Manage server**.

Everything is in your browser. There is no remote desktop, and you do not install the server on your own computer.

## 2. Start the server

Press **Start** on the **Console** tab and wait until the console stops scrolling. New worlds are made on the first start.

## 3. Join

1. Copy the **IP address** and **port** from the panel.
2. In Minecraft, press **Play**, open **Servers**, scroll to the bottom and choose **Add Server**.
3. Put the address and port in their own boxes, then save.

Consoles cannot add servers directly, see [Join from Xbox, PlayStation or Switch](/games/minecraft-bedrock/consoles).

## 4. Make yourself an operator

In the **Console**, with no slash:

```
op "YourGamertag"
```

To use commands such as `/gamemode`, stop the server, set `allow-cheats=true` in `server.properties`, and start it. Cheats permanently turn off achievements for that world. See [Bedrock admin](/games/minecraft-bedrock/admin).

## 5. Make it private

Run `allowlist on` and then `allowlist add YourGamertag` in the **Console**.

## 6. Bring your world and add-ons

- Upload a world from Windows, or a `.mcworld` file: see [Bedrock worlds](/games/minecraft-bedrock/worlds-and-packs).
- Add behavior and resource packs: see [Bedrock Addons in the panel](/games/minecraft-bedrock/bedrock-addons).

## Common problems

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Only one player can join, or a "Door" or NetherNet error | Vanilla Bedrock needs one port per player online. Keep your block of player ports. See [NetherNet](/games/minecraft-bedrock/connecting#nethernet). |
| "Outdated server" after a Minecraft update | Your server only downloads Bedrock when it is installed. Press **Reinstall Server** under **Settings** after a backup. |
| Some players can join and others cannot | Have them update Minecraft. See [Join your Bedrock server](/games/minecraft-bedrock/connecting). |
| Add-ons do nothing | The pack may not match your version or needs experiments on. See [Bedrock Addons](/games/minecraft-bedrock/bedrock-addons). |

## Keep it safe

- Set a backup schedule in the **Backups** tab, and make a backup before any big change. See [Backups](/using_the_panel/backups/guide).
- Big uploads: one upload through the panel can be up to 1 GB on Budget+ and 100 GB on Premium+. Use [SFTP](/using_the_panel/sftp) for anything bigger. Your SFTP password is your game panel password.
- Wrong game or version? You can change it yourself in **Settings**, **Change Game**.

## Still stuck?

Open a ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with your panel link and the error from the **Console**. See [How to get support](/general/getting-support). Never post your password in a ticket.
