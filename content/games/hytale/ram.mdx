---
title: "How much RAM does a Hytale server need?"
description: "Rough RAM guidance by player count and what makes usage grow."
---

These are rough estimates, not official numbers. Start lower and watch usage in the [panel](https://panel.atbphosting.com).

| Players | Approximate RAM |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 5 | 4 to 6 GB |
| 5 to 15 | 6 to 8 GB |
| 20 or more | 10 GB or more |

## What raises usage

- More players, because each loads terrain and inventory.
- Scripts, plugins and complex game modes.
- A longer view distance, which keeps more of the world loaded.

Hytale plans come in every size from 6 to 32 GB on Premium+ (Ryzen 9 9950X), each with 8 GB of NVMe per 1 GB of RAM and backups. Prices are on [Premium+ plans and prices](/plans/premium-plus-plans-and-prices).
