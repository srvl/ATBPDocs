---
title: "Adding an icon"
---

1. Grab the picture you want to use and head over to http://www.genfavicon.com/
2. Paste the image URL link or click choose a file to import a photo from your computer. Then click on the “Upload Image” button.
3. Your image will appear in the upper right-hand corner of the page.
4. Click and drag the little boxes to fit the part of the image you want to include.
5. When you’re satisfied with the selection, head down to the green box that says “cut and preview”. Under this heading, select a size in the dropdown menu. Select 64x64 then click capture and preview.
6. After clicking you should get an option saying “download png”. Click on it and save the file as “server-icon.png”.
7. Go to your server in the [panel](https://panel.atbphosting.com) and open the “File Manager” tab.
8. Click upload and pick the icon you made. Make sure you name the file exactly server-icon.png or this will not work.

Now, restart your server. Feel free to open a ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) if you need any assistance.
