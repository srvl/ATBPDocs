---
title: "Connection Timed Out or Refused"
description: "Fix Connection timed out, Connection refused and getsockopt errors when joining your server."
---

These errors all mean your game could not reach the server:

- **Connection refused** (often shown as `Connection refused: getsockopt`): your game reached the address, but nothing answered on that port. Usually the port is missing or wrong, or the server is not running.
- **Connection timed out**: your game got no answer at all in time. Usually the server is offline, or the route between the player and the server is the problem.

## Check the basics

1. **Is the server online?** Open the [panel](https://panel.atbphosting.com) and confirm the status is running and the console shows the server has finished starting (for Minecraft Java, the `Done` line). If it is stopped, crashed or still starting, fix that first.
2. **Use the full address with the port.** ATBP servers do not use the default port 25565, so the port is needed. Copy the IP and port exactly from the **Network** tab, in the form `IP:port`. An address without `:port` gives **Connection refused**. A subdomain from the panel already includes the port.
3. **Use Direct Connection.** In Minecraft, choose **Direct Connection** instead of a saved server and paste the address. Saved entries keep an old port.
4. **Did you change ports?** If you deleted your main port or made another port primary, the old address stops working. Players need the new one, and a subdomain must be made again on the new port, see [Adding Ports](/using_the_panel/adding-ports). For Geyser, Java and Bedrock players use different ports, see [GeyserMC](/plugins_and_modifications/plugins/geysermc).
5. **Restart the server** from the panel and try again after it fully starts.
6. **Check your version.** See [Outdated Server or Client](/running_a_server/troubleshooting/outdated-server-client).

If the console shows players connecting and then being kicked with a message, the connection works and the problem is the kick reason, see [Kick and Disconnect Messages](/running_a_server/troubleshooting/kick-messages) and [Failed to Verify Username](/running_a_server/troubleshooting/failed-to-verify-username).

## If it works for others but not you

Your own internet or route may be the problem. Signs: the server works on your phone but not on your computer, or the game panel itself will not load for you while it loads for others. This is a routing problem between your internet provider and our DDoS protection, and it is usually temporary.

- Open https://atbphosting.com/network/ to see live whether your ISP's route to our servers is slow or down.
- Restart your router or try mobile data.
- Try a free VPN such as the **1.1.1.1** app (https://one.one.one.one/) on the device that cannot connect. If it works with the VPN on, the route is the cause; use the VPN until it clears. If you already use a VPN, turn it off or try another.
- If it lasts more than a day, run a latency test (see [Diagnosing Ping Issues](/extras/ping-issues)) and send the results with your public IPv4 address in a ticket.

## If the IP and port work but the domain or subdomain does not

Try the numeric address and port from the **Network** tab. If that works, the problem is the domain, or your internet provider's DNS or route to it:

- Check the domain's record, see [Custom Domain](/running_a_server/domain) and [Custom Domains and Subdomains](/general/custom-domains-and-subdomains).
- Try another DNS or a free VPN for a few minutes. If the player can join through a VPN, their provider's route is the cause, and the VPN is a workaround until it clears.

## If only some players cannot join

- If they share one ISP, check https://atbphosting.com/network/ for that ISP. A routing problem inside an ISP has to be fixed by the ISP; a VPN or another connection usually works around it meanwhile.
- Our network protection can block individual IP addresses. Ask an affected player for their public IPv4 address (from any "what is my IP" site) and a few minutes of MTR results, see [Diagnosing Ping Issues](/extras/ping-issues), then open a ticket with both so we can check.
- Ask them to test on mobile data or another network. If it works there, their ISP route is the cause.
- Check https://status.atbphosting.com for regional network incidents.

## If nobody can join

- Look at the console for crashes or errors.
- Make sure a custom domain points to the right address, see [Custom Domain](/running_a_server/domain).

Still cannot connect? Open a [support ticket](/general/getting-support) with your panel link, the exact address players use and your log, see [Share Your Server Logs](/running_a_server/share-logs).
