---
title: "CPU Limit Dropped to Half"
description: "Why the panel's CPU maximum suddenly shows half of your plan (for example 100% instead of 200%), when it comes back, and how to stop it happening again."
---

## How you notice it

- The CPU graph in the game panel tops out at half of what it used to. A 2 vCPU plan that showed `/200%` now shows `/100%`; a 2.5 vCPU plan shows about `/125%`.
- TPS drops and players feel lag at busy times, even though nothing in the server changed today.

In the panel, 100% CPU is one vCPU fully in use, so a 2 vCPU plan normally goes up to 200%.

## What happened

This is CPU Guardian. A server that runs at its full CPU limit for about an hour without a break is throttled automatically to half its CPU, so it cannot slow down the other servers on the node. Short spikes, such as the burst while a server starts or generates a few chunks, do not count.

The full limit comes back by itself at 1:00 AM Philippine time, or straight away if you upgrade the plan. You do not need to do anything for that.

If the CPU graph was only reaching half of the limit and you still lag, the server was probably throttled earlier. A server that keeps hitting the limit will lag at busy times anyway, throttled or not.

## Find what is using the CPU

**Minecraft Java (Paper, Purpur, Fabric and others):**

1. While players are online and the lag is happening, type `spark profiler start --timeout 120` in the **Console** (no `/` in the console; in game it is `/spark`). See [Spark](/running_a_server/spark).
2. Open the link it gives you, or paste it into the [Spark Analyzer](/running_a_server/spark-analyzer).
3. The usual causes: view and simulation distance set too high, an unexplored world being generated as players walk, large mob or villager farms, and datapacks. Datapacks run much slower than a plugin or mod that does the same job, so swap them for one where you can. Take a backup before removing a datapack, because some leave the world broken without them.

**Minecraft Bedrock:**

Spark does not run on Bedrock. Lower `view-distance` and `tick-distance` in `server.properties`, and check heavy add-ons. If you run Vanilla Bedrock, switching to Endstone uses less CPU for the same players and keeps your world; see [Endstone or Vanilla Bedrock?](/games/minecraft-bedrock/endstone-vs-vanilla).

**Other games:** lower the player count, view distance or tick rate in the game's own settings, and check mods you added recently.

Tuning tips for Minecraft are on [Optimization](/running_a_server/optimization) and [Troubleshooting lag](/running_a_server/troubleshooting/lag-troubleshooting).

## Get the limit lifted early

Once you have fixed the cause, open a ticket in our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with your panel link and staff can lift the limit before 1:00 AM. Your hour then starts again from zero, and if the server goes straight back to full CPU the limit can come back.

## If it keeps happening

If a tuned server still runs at its CPU limit every evening, the plan has too few vCPU for the players and mods you run. Upgrading gives the full limit back at once and keeps your files. See [Upgrades and downgrades](/billing/upgrades-and-downgrades) and [Choosing a plan](/general/choosing-a-plan). Staff can look at your server in a ticket and tell you whether tuning or a bigger plan will help more.
