---
title: "Share Your Server Logs"
description: "How to share your console log safely when asking for help."
---

When you ask staff or other server owners for help, they usually need your log. Pasting a huge log into chat is messy, so upload it instead.

## Upload it from the panel

1. Open your server's **MC Logs** page in the panel.
2. Pick the log: `latest.log` is your current or most recent run, older ones are the dated `.gz` files.
3. Press **Upload to mclo.gs**, copy the link it gives you and send it in your [support ticket](/general/getting-support).

![The panel's MC Logs page with latest.log and the upload button marked](/screenshots/panel-more-mclogs.webp)


## Or do it by hand

1. Open the **Files** tab and go to the `logs` folder, or copy text straight from the **Console** tab if the problem just happened.
2. Paste the text or upload the file at [mclo.gs](https://mclo.gs), which is made for Minecraft logs, and save.
3. Copy the link and send it in your ticket.

## Before you share

Logs can include player names and IP addresses. Good log sites hide IPs automatically, but check for passwords, tokens or anything private. Never post your panel login or API keys.
