---
title: "Reset the Nether or End"
description: "Regenerate the Nether or End without touching your main world."
---

import { Callout } from 'nextra/components'

Resetting a dimension removes everything built there, such as portals and bases. Your Overworld is not affected, but your portals back may need to be rebuilt.

<Callout type="warning">

Create a [backup](/using_the_panel/backups/guide) and stop the server before deleting anything.

</Callout>

Stop the server and open the **Files** tab, then delete the folder for your software and version. Your main world folder is named after `level-name` in `server.properties` (usually `world`).

| Your server | Nether | End |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper, Purpur or Spigot on 1.21.11 or older | `world_nether` | `world_the_end` |
| Paper or Purpur on 26.1 or newer | `world/dimensions/minecraft/the_nether` | `world/dimensions/minecraft/the_end` |
| Vanilla, Fabric, Forge or NeoForge on 1.21.11 or older | `world/DIM-1` | `world/DIM1` |
| Vanilla, Fabric, Forge or NeoForge on 26.1 or newer | `world/dimensions/minecraft/the_nether` | `world/dimensions/minecraft/the_end` |

On Paper 26.1 or newer, also delete `world_nether` or `world_the_end` if one is still next to `world`, or Paper may move the old dimension back in on the next start.

Start the server. A fresh dimension is generated the first time someone enters it.

To reset everything, see [Resetting your Worlds](/running_a_server/world-reset).
